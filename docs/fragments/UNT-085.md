### UNT-085 - The hour is a blend, not a branch

- Routing class: `feature` -> **claude-sonnet-5**, effort **medium**. Bias 1. Implementation to a spec that already answers the design questions. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened for: [[RLG-091]]
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-08-30T22:41:40-04:00** - The hour is a blend rather than three branches. Only the GROUND was snapping: nightFall and goldenHour have always returned smooth ramps and every sky stop already mixes against them, but the ground tone, the sea and the mirror's snow each thresholded the same numbers at > 0.5 and > 0.25 and flipped between three fixed looks in one frame. A smooth sky over a ground that jumps is why it read as the whole world jumping. Night is applied after gold so full night still wins, which is what the old ordering did at its extremes. Measured across a day in 360 steps: the worst single frame carried 100 per cent of the ground's colour travel at phase 0.128 - where nightFall crosses 0.5 - against 3 per cent after. hour-test compares the step with the day's own range rather than a brightness number, because a fixed threshold would need re-tuning whenever a biome palette changed; and sampling four times a day, which is what a screenshot pass does, would have found three plausible colours and no snap, since the fault lived only between the samples.

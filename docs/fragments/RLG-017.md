### RLG-017 — Interstate: snow followed you into the desert
- **RULED 2026-08-28T13:40:16-04:00** — - **Owner report:** if a weather effect should not exist in the next biome, it has to end immediately.
  Snow was carrying over into the desert.

- **The cause is a one-way use of the odds.** Each biome carries a chance of rain and a chance of
  snow, and `stepWeather` rolls against them **when it picks a front**. Nothing ever checked them
  again. A front chosen in a tundra kept falling for the 35 to 80 seconds left on its own timer,
  wherever the road went in the meantime - so the biome decided what MIGHT start and never what had
  to stop.

- **The settled ground was the worse half.** Snow whitens the ground as it lands and fades at a rate
  of 0.03, which is about a minute and a half. Driving out of a tundra left the desert white long
  after the fall had stopped.

- **The fix, and where the line is drawn.** A biome that CANNOT produce what is falling ends it now:
  the target goes to zero, the next roll is brought forward so the new place gets its own weather
  soon, and settled snow melts at 0.55 instead of 0.03. **Rain in a desert is rare rather than
  impossible, so it is left to run itself out** - only a zero chance is treated as a contradiction.
  That keeps the odds meaning what they say instead of becoming a hard filter.

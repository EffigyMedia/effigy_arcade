### RLG-133 — The checkpoint boards vanish from the mirror, and the finish line was never in it
- **RULED 2026-08-31T21:04:31-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-08-31, after playing through all the tournaments: "I still don't think I see the back of
the checkpoints or the finish line in the rearview mirror."

"STILL" IS THE IMPORTANT WORD AND THE REPORT IS RIGHT TWICE OVER. [[RLG-108]] shipped a mirror pass
for the checkpoint boards and the owner is looking at a build that has it. THERE ARE TWO DIFFERENT
FAULTS BEHIND ONE SENTENCE, and naming the finish line separately is what separates them - the same
lesson [[RLG-041]] took eleven versions to learn, that the detail in a device report is the
diagnosis.

FAULT ONE - THE CHECKPOINT BOARDS ARE DRAWN AND THEN DELETED ALMOST IMMEDIATELY. The mirror pass
works. What defeats it is one line in the checkpoint stepper:

    cpGantries = cpGantries.filter(cp => cp.z > pos - 8000);

A board is thrown away 8,000 units after it is passed. THE MIRROR CAN SEE 34,000, so the board is
culled at less than a quarter of the range the glass has - and RLG-108's own comment says the
retention was "already right", which was the mistake. In distance and time:

    8,000 units    = 0.062 of a mile, about 100 metres
    at 150mph      = ABOUT ONE AND A HALF SECONDS

So the board exists in the mirror for a second and a half after you pass under it, during which it is
shrinking fast, in a pane 44 pixels tall. The owner is not failing to notice it; it is very nearly
not there. The fix is the retention, not the painter: 34,000 to match what the glass can draw.

FAULT TWO - THE FINISH LINE WAS NEVER IN THE MIRROR AT ALL. `drawFinish` is a forward-view painter
and nothing else calls it. It returns early on `gap < -600`, so it stops 600 units past the line -
about eight metres - and there is no equivalent of `drawGantryBack` for it. RLG-108 was scoped to
`cpGantries` and the finish banner is a separate object with a separate painter, so it was never in
that unit. This one needs a back-facing painter written, not a number changed.

AND CROSSING THE FINISH IS THE ONE MOMENT A PLAYER MOST WANTS THE MIRROR, which is worth saying out
loud: it is the only time in a race when what is behind you is the whole story.

TWO THINGS TO GET RIGHT WHEN IT IS BUILT:

  THE BACK OF A BOARD IS NOT ITS FRONT. `drawGantryBack` exists because a checkpoint sign reads one
  way round; a chequered finish banner is closer to symmetrical, but its lettering and any sponsor
  boards are not, and drawing the front of it in the mirror is the same defect in a new place.

  RAISING THE RETENTION COSTS A LITTLE EVERY FRAME. `cpGantries` is walked for the hit test as well
  as for drawing, so keeping boards four times as long lengthens that walk. It is a handful of
  objects and almost certainly free, but it should be said rather than assumed.

AND THERE IS A CHECK-SHAPED HOLE HERE. `API.putGantry` places a board at 4,000 units behind by
default - INSIDE the 8,000 that survives - so a harness asking "is a board in the mirror" got yes on
a build where a real one had already gone. A check that walked out to 20,000 would have caught this.

NOT STARTED.

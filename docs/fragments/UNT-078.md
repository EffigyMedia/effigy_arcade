### UNT-078 - A short gearbox is designed, not a six-speed with the top gears cut off

- Routing class: `feature` -> **claude-sonnet-5**, effort **medium**. Bias 1. Implementation to a spec that already answers the design questions. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened for: [[RLG-069]]
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-08-30T20:06:06-04:00** - A short gearbox is generated from the six-speed's shift curve instead of being the six-speed with its top gears cut off and the last one stretched to the top. The muscle car's fourth ran 0.41 to 1.00, fifty-nine per cent of the range in one gear against 0.17 in first, which is the grind the owner reported. Four-speed bands are now 0.241, 0.277, 0.322, 0.335 with the top gear at 34 per cent; five-speed 0.200 to 0.284 with the top at 28. A six-speed is returned untouched. The check is the ratio of the longest gear to the shortest, not that the bands grow - they grew on the broken table too, so a growth assertion would have passed the build being complained about. With the truncated table put back, gate-test reads 3.47 for every four-speed and 2.47 for every five-speed against a limit of 1.6. The gate half of RLG-069 was already built and green. Whether the muscle car now FEELS right is the owner's on the device.

### RLG-032 — Interstate: traffic goes round slower cars instead of queueing behind them
- **RULED 2026-08-28T15:25:25-04:00** — - **Owner request:** traffic intelligence - merging to go around slower cars.

- **What civilians could already do, and it was only half a driver.** They read the road ahead and
  SLOWED for it: a roadblock, a slower car in their own tyre tracks, and the player. That was the
  entire repertoire. Nothing on the motorway ever considered the lane beside it, so a car queued
  behind a slower one forever and the road silted up into rolling walls.

- **A merge is a decision, not a drift.** It is made once, committed to, and carried out over about
  two seconds, which is why it lives in state on the car rather than being re-derived every frame.
  **A car that re-decides at 60Hz weaves**, and weaving reads as a bug even when every individual
  frame is defensible.

- **The test for a safe lane is the one a driver actually makes**, and it is asked about a LATERAL
  POSITION rather than a lane index - because a lane index is an assignment and the road is a width.
  Is there a hole beside me; will it still be a hole when I arrive; is anything closing on it fast
  from behind. The window is deliberately asymmetric - longer forward, because we are moving into
  somebody's braking distance, shorter behind, because a car back there can lift.

- **And it only pulls out if the other lane is FASTER.** Moving over to sit beside the car you were
  already following is worse than staying put, so the gain has to clear a margin before the decision
  is taken.

- **The player is a car like any other in both tests.** Traffic that merges through you is worse than
  traffic that never merges at all.

- **TWO BUGS, ONE FOUND BY READING AND ONE BY THE HARNESS.**
  1. `c.mergeCool <= 0` on a freshly spawned car is `undefined <= 0`, which is **false** - so no car
     would ever have merged until something set the field, and nothing ever would. Written as
     `!(c.mergeCool > 0)`.
  2. Merging immediately broke the pileup guarantee from [[RLG-025]]: the narrowest corridor went from
     0.452 to **0.245**, under the limit. A car had found a hole, moved into it, and **the hole was
     the way through.** `keepLaneOpen` would have re-opened it eventually, but "eventually" is not
     what an absolute guarantee means, and undoing a merge a second after making it is the weaving
     this design set out to avoid. So the check happens before the decision: **a car does not take
     the last gap.**

- **Measured after both fixes:** 37 merges over a 45-second run, narrowest corridor **0.418** against
  the 0.34 limit, 0 of 180 samples blocked. `drive-test` 21/21.

- **The indicator is not decoration.** A car blinks for the beat before it moves across. Traffic that
  changes lane without warning is what makes a road feel malicious rather than busy.

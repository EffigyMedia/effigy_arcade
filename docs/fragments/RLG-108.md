### RLG-108 — Checkpoint gantries are missing from the rear-view
- **RULED 2026-08-31T10:50:58-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-31: "Checkpoint signs aren't shown in the rearview mirror by the way."

IT IS THE SAME GAP [[RLG-079]] CLOSED FOR EVERYTHING ELSE. The mirror was given the sky, the ground,
the tarmac, the biome, the weather, the scenery, the treeline, the cars, their headlights and their
wipers - each time by asking the SAME function the windscreen asks. The checkpoint gantries were never
carried across. They are road furniture standing in the world, and the mirror is a picture of the
world behind the car.

AND A CHECKPOINT IS THE ONE PIECE OF FURNITURE WITH A REASON TO BE IN THERE. Every other thing in the
glass is scenery or traffic. A gantry is the thing the Interstate is played against: a player who is
not sure whether they have crossed one has a real question, and the mirror is where the answer is.
That makes this more than a completeness item.

WHERE IT GOES. `cpGantries` is the list, it is already filtered to `cp.z > pos - 8000` so boards
BEHIND the car are deliberately kept for a while, and the mirror can already see 34,000 units back.
So the data is there and the retention is right - what is missing is a pass. The mirror's own sprite
loop is where it belongs, drawn with the same `rproj` everything else in the glass uses.

TWO THINGS TO GET RIGHT. A gantry seen from behind is not the same picture as one seen from in front -
whatever the forward painter does with a face, the rear of it is a different drawing, and this project
has a standing rule that a machine must not fade to a simplified render. And it is a TALL object: the
mirror's projection pushes near things off the bottom edge, and a gantry spans the road, so it will
be clipped by the pane's top and bottom in a way a car never is.

MEASURE IT THE WAY THE MIRROR'S OTHER GAPS WERE MEASURED. Not by a screenshot: park a board at a known
distance behind the car with `API.gantries()` already reporting how many exist, and assert the glass
gets busier when one is there than when it is not. That is the shape `mirror-rain-test` uses and it
falsifies cleanly.

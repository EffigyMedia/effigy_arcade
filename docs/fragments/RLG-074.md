### RLG-074 — Every racer in the mirror was a drawn block
- **RULED 2026-08-29T10:23:27-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-29, from the device: all the racers show the old generic rearview render.

They did. The mirror draws a simplified block for a distant car and fades the real painted nose in
over it as the car gets close, and the nose is looked up in `FRONT_SP`, which is keyed by TRAFFIC
TYPE - 'sedan', 'coupe', 'van'. A racer has no `type`: it carries a body key and a paint. So the
lookup found nothing, the fade never started, and every rival stayed a coloured lozenge however close
it came - while the delivery van beside it had a painted face.

`rivalFront(body, paint)` builds the missing sprite through the same two painters everything else
uses, and the mirror asks for it when the car it is drawing has a `body` rather than a `type`.

BUILT ON DEMAND, and that is deliberate. The rears are cached for every rival body in every paint -
nine bodies by twelve colours, 108 canvases - and doing the same for the fronts would put 216 on a
phone for cars that mostly never appear behind the player. A race puts about eight rivals on the
road, so this builds about eight. `null` is cached as well as a sprite, so a body that cannot produce
a front is not retried sixty times a second.

Two vehicles still have no face in the mirror and it is worth saying so rather than leaving it to be
discovered: the POLICE cars, which are excluded by an older `it.cop` test, and the LORRY and the CAB
among the traffic, whose fronts are not in the `FRONT_SP` build loop. Both are the same shape of gap
as this one.

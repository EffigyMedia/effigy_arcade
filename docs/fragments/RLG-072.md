### RLG-072 — The garage shows both ends of the car
- **RULED 2026-08-29T09:53:18-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-29: the garage shows the front and the back of the selected car, even at the cost of
showing each smaller.

Only the tail was ever built for the player's car, because the tail is the only end you see while
driving. The garage is the one screen where the player looks AT the car instead of following it, and
a car you have never seen the face of is half a car - which mattered more the moment every front in
the fleet was drawn and wired (RLG-053).

`buildSprites` now builds `SP.playerFront` beside `SP.player`, through the same two painters the
fleet sheet uses: `paintRigFront` at the rig's box for a road car, `paintFront` at 230x215 for
everything else. Built once per change of car or paint rather than per frame of a menu.

The card draws both ends into their own half of the canvas, standing on one floor line, so they read
as one car rather than as two pictures. Each fell from 240 pixels wide to about 126, which is the
price the owner accepted. The canvas lost fifty pixels of height with it: two cars side by side are
limited by the width of their half, so a 180-tall card left a band of nothing above them, and on a
phone that band is scroll.

`showUnlock` draws through the same function, so a newly won car is also shown from both ends without
that screen knowing anything about it.

### RLG-019 — Interstate: racers drove through the player
- **RULED 2026-08-28T13:41:40-04:00** — - **Owner report:** racers and the player do not collide.

- **Measured, and it is exactly that.** `stepRacers` collides a rival against traffic, against
  cruisers, against roadblocks and against other rivals. It never once looks at the player. **Every
  other body on the road was solid to a racer except the one the player is sitting in**, so a rival
  could hold your lane and share your bumper for a mile.

- **It is a rub, not a wreck, and that is a design choice rather than a shortcut.** A rival is racing
  you - leaning on each other is the sport. Spinning a rival on every touch would empty the field in
  the first mile, and spinning the PLAYER on every touch would make the pack unapproachable. So both
  cars lose speed, both are pushed apart, and the shunt scales with the closing speed: brushing a car
  going your pace barely registers, and arriving sixty miles an hour faster throws you both.

- **Only the player takes damage, because the player is the only one keeping a health bar.** It is 8
  rather than traffic's 13 - contact with something racing in the same direction is a rub, not the
  T-bone that hitting a slow-moving civilian is.

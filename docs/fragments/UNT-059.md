### UNT-059 - The title card's car is the engine's own car

- Routing class: `fix` -> **claude-sonnet-5**, effort **medium**. Bias 1. A known defect with a known cause. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-08-30T10:36:54-04:00** - The title screen was the surface, settled by capturing both it and the launcher's card: the card is a 90-pixel thumbnail where a car is a trapezoid, and the title carries the engine's own sprite. The car there was always the right one - what was wrong is that the title painted two red rectangles at hand-written coordinates over a sprite that already knew where its lamps were. It survived because lampsHere painted into the road's own context and there was no way to tell it otherwise; one argument fixes that, and every surface that draws a car can now light it from the one declaration. The check strips the declaration at runtime and looks again, which is the only way to tell a declared lamp from a painted one: 550 red pixels lit, 0 stripped, and 1,253 stripped when the rectangles are put back.

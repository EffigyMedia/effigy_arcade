### RLG-105 — A tunnel, where it is dark by day
- **RULED 2026-08-31T10:50:57-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-31, answering [[RLG-102]]: canyon and tunnel are the two to build.

NOTHING ON THE BOARD IS DARK BY DAY, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE POINT. Weather and the hour drive the
light, and every place is lit the same way at the same hour. A tunnel breaks that: the sky is gone,
the daylight is gone, and the only light is what the road and the cars bring with them.

IT MAKES WORK THAT IS ALREADY BUILT EARN ITS KEEP. The headlight beams, the tail lamps, every front
and rear sprite's lamp declaration from [[RLG-053]], and the street lighting all exist and currently
only matter after dark. `lampsOn()` is the one clock they follow, and it reads the hour. A tunnel is
the first thing that would make it read the PLACE as well - which is a small change to one function
and a large change to what the game looks like at midday.

SO `lampsOn` IS THE SEAM, AND IT SHOULD STAY ONE FUNCTION. Every lamp in the game asks it. A tunnel
that lit its cars by a second route would be two answers to one question, and this file has the
receipts on what that costs. The place should contribute a darkness the way it contributes rain.

THE HORIZON IS THE HARD PART. A tunnel has no sky, no skyline and no far ground - the vanishing point
is a dark mouth rather than a horizon line, and `drawSky`, `groundBase` and `buildSkyline` all assume
there is something up there. This is the opposite problem to the canyon's and it needs the same
decision made first: what does the far field draw when the place has no distance in it.

AND THE ENTRANCE AND EXIT ARE THE FEATURE. Driving into darkness and back out into daylight is the
moment worth building; a tunnel that is simply a dark corridor for two minutes is a place with one
idea and no event in it. The transition machinery already exists - a biome arrives at the horizon and
drives toward you ([[RLG-022]]) - so the mouth can be the boundary rather than a special case.

A COMFORT NOTE, BECAUSE IT IS THE SAME HAZARD AS THE LIGHTNING. [[RLG-060]] treats the storm flash as a
photosensitivity risk and gives it an option. A tunnel's exit is a large sudden brightening at speed,
repeated every time the place ends. It should be a ramp rather than a cut, and it should be judged on
the device.

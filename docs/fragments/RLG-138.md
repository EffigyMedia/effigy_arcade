### RLG-138 — The turn cautionary signs are removed
- **RULED 2026-09-01T00:38:06-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-09-01: "We can completely remove the turn cautionary signs."

DONE, AND THE REASONING THAT PUT THEM THERE IS LEFT IN PLACE RATHER THAN DELETED. The comment in the generator says a bend you cannot see coming is a trap rather than a corner, and that the boards are straight from Out Run and the reason its corners feel fair at speed. None of that was wrong when it was written.

WHAT CHANGED IS THE ROAD UNDER IT. The corner cap in this engine is a renderer limit rather than a taste one - past about ninety degrees the road leaves the frame - and since RLG-059 every biome scales its bends by its own sinuosity, with MOUNTAIN at 1.00 being the road as it always was and everywhere else calmer. The hardest corner the generator can now produce is a good deal gentler than the one those boards were written to warn about. A sign warning of a hairpin the road can no longer make is furniture.

THE SPAWN IS REMOVED AND THE PAINTER IS NOT. `drawSign` and its sprite stay exactly where they are, the array is still walked and still culled, and the five lines that pushed a board are gone. Nothing downstream had to change and the boards come back by restoring those five lines. Deleting a painter to remove a feature is how a feature stops being recoverable, and this store has receipts on that.

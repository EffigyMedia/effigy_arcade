### UNT-081 - Three, two, one, GO before the car moves

- Routing class: `feature` -> **claude-sonnet-5**, effort **medium**. Bias 1. Implementation to a spec that already answers the design questions. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened for: [[RLG-088]]
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-08-30T20:30:40-04:00** - Hitting DRIVE gives a three, two, one, GO countdown. The car is HELD rather than covered: step() returns before speed, the run clock, distance or score are touched, while everything drawn afterwards keeps running, so the player looks down the road they are about to take. Neither mode had a standing start, so one count-in serves both and there was no circuit sequence to reconcile. The flare is timing rather than size - each number arrives at 1.35 and settles, a ring leaves it as it lands, and GO is bigger, green, lifts and stays up while the car is already moving. The sound is the opposite of the run-out tick: pips climbing a whole tone each, and a major chord on GO. After the first run the throttle shortens the count to a third of a second rather than cancelling it. THE CHECK HOLDS THE PEDAL DOWN FROM BEFORE THE COUNT BEGINS - a car nobody is asking to move sits still whether it is held or not, and the first version passed on a build with no countdown at all. Removing the hold fails three checks together. Whether the flare LANDS is the owner's on the device.

### UNT-080 - The garage card has two sizes: the regular cars, and the big ones

- Routing class: `feature` -> **claude-sonnet-5**, effort **medium**. Bias 1. Implementation to a spec that already answers the design questions. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened for: [[RLG-087]]
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-08-30T20:19:27-04:00** - The garage card has two sizes instead of one: the ordinary cars reserve 112 px and the two oversized vehicles reserve 150. One reservation made every car pay for the lorry - the fleet runs LORRY 150, VAN 115, then PICKUP 112 down to ROADSTER 78, so a roadster sat above 72 px of air for a vehicle most players never choose. Air above a roadster is now 34 px, a card still never changes size between two ordinary cars, and it does change when you scroll onto something the size of a lorry - which reads as the vehicle being different. The oversized flag is declared on the body rather than inferred from a height, because a threshold rule would silently re-tier a car the day somebody adjusted a sprite. The check asks for exactly the tallest of each tier AND that the two tiers differ; collapsing them back to one fails both.

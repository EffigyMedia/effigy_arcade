### RLG-054 — Three driving personalities for every NPC: Racer, Speeder, Civilian
- **RULED 2026-08-28T19:52:34-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-28:** "I want all NPC cars to have driving personalities in 3 categories: Racer,
Speeder, and Civilian."

**Every NPC on the road gets one.** Rivals and traffic alike - this is one system, not a traffic
feature. That is the same shape as the owner's other rulings this week, and by now it is the
project's spine rather than a coincidence: **the CAR is fully capable, the DRIVER decides.** It is
[[RLG-042]]'s "traffic cars have real stats, their drivers are the limitation" and [[RLG-052]]'s
"every vehicle's indicators are wired, nothing asks them to come on", applied to behaviour.

| personality | what it does |
|---|---|
| **Civilian** | keeps the limit - 70-80mph per [[RLG-045]] |
| **Speeder** | exceeds it, by choice |
| **Racer** | races |

**Owner's answers, same day, to the two questions this fragment put:**

1. **A Racer appears in traffic, rarely.** Not reserved for the tournament field. So the three are a
   real spectrum rather than two traffic types plus a label for rivals, and the engine's existing
   "rogue" - one in five tuners or muscle cars cruising at 100-124mph, spawned that way at
   road.js:4060 - becomes the TOP of that spectrum instead of a separate mechanism bolted to the
   spawner. **The rogue is superseded by this, not kept alongside it.**
2. **The Civilian/Speeder split stays at about 90/10**, as [[RLG-045]] already ruled, with a sports
   car raising the odds of a driver being a Speeder. So this ruling REFINES RLG-045 rather than
   replacing it: RLG-045 owns the limit and the mix, this owns the categories.

**A traffic Racer is worth more than the surprise it gives the player.** [[RLG-046]] has the police
engaging any car that speeds, and [[RLG-047]] has non-racers pulling over. Both need something on the
road genuinely worth chasing that is NOT the player, or they can only ever be tested against the
player and will be built to that one case. A traffic Racer is exactly that, and it should exist
before either of them is finished.

**What "personality" must NOT become.** A category is a set of DECISIONS - what speed to hold,
whether to overtake, whether to signal ([[RLG-052]]), whether to pull over ([[RLG-047]]). It is not a
second stat table and it must not adjust the car's capability. [[RLG-042]] rules one table for every
vehicle; a personality that quietly makes a car faster would reintroduce the exact fault that
measurement just found, in a new place and under a friendlier name.

**Open, and it needs answering before this is built:** does a personality show on the car? The owner
has ruled colour is a CLASS signal in [[RLG-044]] - racers saturated, traffic muted - and a traffic
Racer is a traffic car with a racer's personality, so under RLG-044 it wears muted paint. That is
probably right, and it is the sort of thing that should be decided rather than fallen into: the
player learns a fast car in muted colours is not a rival, which is a lesson worth teaching once.

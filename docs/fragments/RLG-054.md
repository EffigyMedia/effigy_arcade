### RLG-054 — Three driving personalities for every NPC: Racer, Speeder, Civilian
- **RULED 2026-08-28T19:52:34-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-28:** "I want all NPC cars to have driving personalities in 3 categories: Racer,
Speeder, and Civilian."

**Every NPC on the road gets one.** Rivals and traffic alike - this is one system, not a traffic
feature. That is the same shape as the owner's other rulings this week, and by now it is the
project's spine rather than a coincidence: **the CAR is fully capable, the DRIVER decides.** It is
[[RLG-042]]'s "traffic cars have real stats, their drivers are the limitation" and [[RLG-052]]'s
"every vehicle's indicators are wired, nothing asks them to come on", applied to behaviour.

| personality | what it does |
|---|---|
| **Civilian** | keeps the limit - 70-80mph per [[RLG-045]] |
| **Speeder** | exceeds it, by choice |
| **Racer** | races |

**Owner's answers, same day, to the two questions this fragment put:**

1. **A Racer appears in traffic, rarely.** Not reserved for the tournament field. So the three are a
   real spectrum rather than two traffic types plus a label for rivals, and the engine's existing
   "rogue" - one in five tuners or muscle cars cruising at 100-124mph, spawned that way at
   road.js:4060 - becomes the TOP of that spectrum instead of a separate mechanism bolted to the
   spawner. **The rogue is superseded by this, not kept alongside it.**
2. **The Civilian/Speeder split stays at about 90/10**, as [[RLG-045]] already ruled, with a sports
   car raising the odds of a driver being a Speeder. So this ruling REFINES RLG-045 rather than
   replacing it: RLG-045 owns the limit and the mix, this owns the categories.

**A traffic Racer is worth more than the surprise it gives the player.** [[RLG-046]] has the police
engaging any car that speeds, and [[RLG-047]] has non-racers pulling over. Both need something on the
road genuinely worth chasing that is NOT the player, or they can only ever be tested against the
player and will be built to that one case. A traffic Racer is exactly that, and it should exist
before either of them is finished.

**What "personality" must NOT become.** A category is a set of DECISIONS - what speed to hold,
whether to overtake, whether to signal ([[RLG-052]]), whether to pull over ([[RLG-047]]). It is not a
second stat table and it must not adjust the car's capability. [[RLG-042]] rules one table for every
vehicle; a personality that quietly makes a car faster would reintroduce the exact fault that
measurement just found, in a new place and under a friendlier name.

**Open, and it needs answering before this is built:** does a personality show on the car? The owner
has ruled colour is a CLASS signal in [[RLG-044]] - racers saturated, traffic muted - and a traffic
Racer is a traffic car with a racer's personality, so under RLG-044 it wears muted paint. That is
probably right, and it is the sort of thing that should be decided rather than fallen into: the
player learns a fast car in muted colours is not a rival, which is a lesson worth teaching once.

> muted colors."

**This lands exactly on the spine this fragment already has, which is why it is recorded here rather
than as its own item.** The three personalities are a spectrum, a Racer appears in traffic rarely,
and [[RLG-045]] already ruled that a sports car raises the odds of a driver being a Speeder. A
supercar in traffic is the top of that same spectrum: **the CAR is fully capable, and the DRIVER is
what makes it traffic.**

**What it asks for, in three parts:**

1. **The supercars enter the traffic pool** - STALLION, MATADOR, CREST and SUPERCRUISER's shape, but
   **not the FORMULA**, which has no business on a public road and no indicators either
   ([[RLG-052]]).
2. **Very very rare.** The owner said it twice, which is the specification. The engine's existing
   rogue is one in five of tuners and muscle cars; this is a different order of magnitude and should
   be written as such rather than folded into the same roll.
3. **In their muted colours, and they INHERIT the raised Speeder chance.** That is [[RLG-044]] and
   RLG-045 meeting: colour is a CLASS signal, so a supercar driven by a civilian wears the muted
   paint of traffic rather than a racer's saturated one - and its driver is likelier than a saloon's
   to be a Speeder because of what they chose to drive.

**The thing to get right, and it is the whole point:** a supercar in traffic must not read as a
rival. It is the same car in traffic paint with a traffic driver, and the only thing that says so is
its COLOUR and its BEHAVIOUR. If it ends up saturated, or racing, the feature has failed - and it
will look like a bug in the rival spawner rather than a deliberate rarity.

**It cannot be built before the personalities are**, because "inherit the increased percentage of
being a speeder" is a sentence about a system that does not exist yet. This fragment is that system.

**Read before starting:** [[RLG-042]]'s ruling that traffic cars have real stats and their drivers
are the limitation. A supercar in traffic gets the supercar's stats. It is slow because the person
driving it is going to work.
- **RULED 2026-08-31T12:06:24-04:00** — BUILT IN 0.10.24, AND THE SHAPE IS THE PART TO KEEP. A personality picks a TARGET SPEED and nothing
else. What the car can actually do is `TYPE_VMAX`, which belongs to the vehicle, and the cruise is the
smaller of the two. That single line is the whole of "the car is capable, the driver decides" made
mechanical, and it is what stops a personality becoming a second stat table.

    cruise = min(what this driver wants, what this vehicle can do)

SO A SPEEDER IN A LORRY IS A SPEEDER IN INTENT AND DRIVES A LORRY. Measured: a truck tops out at 68mph
whoever is behind the wheel, which is UNDER the 80mph limit - so a lorry driver who chooses to speed
never actually speeds, trips no trap and is chased by nobody. A tuner with the same mind reaches
140. Nothing in the code names a body to decide a personality; the body only caps the outcome.

THE MIX, SAMPLED FROM THE ENGINE'S OWN ROLL, 4,000 PER TYPE:

    type      civilian  speeder  racer   the car can do
    sedan        89%      10%     1.1%      116 mph
    van          90%      10%     0.9%      100
    truck        88%      11%     0.9%       68
    taxi         88%      11%     0.9%      110
    pickup       90%       9%     0.8%      104
    coupe        65%      29%     5.5%      132
    tuner        67%      28%     4.6%      148
    muscle       68%      28%     4.6%      156

RLG-045's 90/10 HOLDS EXACTLY on the ordinary bodies, and a sports car lifts the Speeder odds by 2.8
times - which is that ruling's "a sports car raises the odds of a driver being a Speeder" turned into
a number rather than a sentence. A Racer is about one car in fifty overall, which is the owner's
"rarely".

THE ROGUE IS SUPERSEDED AND ITS RATE IS DELIBERATELY UNCHANGED. One in five tuners and muscle cars
used to cruise at 100-124mph under a `rogue` flag - about 2.8% of all traffic. A Racer is about 2%.
That is a supersession rather than an increase, which matters because the owner asked for rarity and a
new mechanism at a new rate would have been two changes wearing one name.

AND THE SAME RELATIONSHIP IS READ IN BOTH DIRECTIONS, which is the neatest part. A car spawned BEHIND
the player has to be quicker than the player or it never arrives, so its speed is decided before its
driver is. Rather than let it carry a personality that contradicts what it is doing - a Civilian at
110 - `mindFor` reads the personality OFF the speed. A driver is what they are doing.

THAT SPAWNER ALSO HAD A BUG THIS FOUND. It capped at a flat 0.46 of top speed for every body, so a
LORRY came up behind the player at 92mph. It takes `TYPE_VMAX` now, like everything else.

WHAT THE TWO READERS OF THE OLD FLAG BECAME. The speed trap and the cruiser's target search both asked
"is this a rogue". They ask "is this a driver who chooses to exceed the limit" now - and both already
had a SPEED test after that question, which is why a Speeder in a lorry correctly trips neither.

MEASURED ON THE ROAD AS WELL AS IN THE ROLL, and the road check had to be rewritten to be worth
anything. Its first version took ONE snapshot: 14 cars, all civilian, zero speeders - and would have
passed on an engine that assigned every driver the same mind, because at one in ten a fourteen-car
sample is empty of speeders about a quarter of the time. It watches 24 times across a drive now and
keeps the most it ever saw: 24 cars, 22 civilian, 2 speeder, and 2 genuinely over the limit. That is
the assertion the sampler cannot make - that the picker is WIRED, not merely correct.

NO REGRESSION IN THE TRAFFIC, AND IT WAS CHECKED ON BOTH ARMS. `traffic-test`'s corridor guarantee
fails about one run in three on this machine, which is RLG-037's known flake. Three runs before this
change: 0.288, 0.347, 0.441. Three runs after: 0.295, 0.409, 0.372. The same distribution and the same
one-in-three, so the traffic speed changes did not move it. `drive-test` is 27 of 27 three times over,
with the Interstate peaking at 151, 153 and 129mph.

STILL OPEN FROM THIS RULING, and deliberately not built here. The rare supercars in traffic - the
owner's extension of 2026-08-29 - now have the system they were waiting for, and they are the obvious
next step: STALLION, MATADOR, CREST and the super cruiser's shape, not the FORMULA, in muted colours,
inheriting the raised Speeder chance. And the open question the fragment put on its first day is still
open: whether a personality should show on the car at all. RLG-044 says colour is a CLASS signal, so a
traffic Racer wears muted paint - which is probably right and is still the owner's to confirm.
- **RULED 2026-08-31T12:27:47-04:00** — BUILT IN 0.10.25, AND THE OWNER SETTLED THE OPEN QUESTION THIS FRAGMENT PUT ON ITS FIRST DAY.
Owner, 2026-08-31: "No personality shows other than their behavior. Traffic racer only wears traffic
paint yes." So the question this ruling raised - does a personality show on the car - is answered NO,
and RLG-044's class signal stands unqualified: colour says CLASS, behaviour says DRIVER, and nothing
says personality.

THE SAME SHAPE, THE SAME PAINTER, A DIFFERENT PALETTE. A traffic supercar is built exactly as a rival
is - `paintCar` with the body's own shape record - with the colour coming from `trafficPaint` instead
of the saturated rival list. That is the whole difference, and it is deliberately the whole
difference.

AND NO STRIPES, WHICH COST NOTHING BECAUSE RIVALS HAD NONE EITHER. `stripes` is an option the painter
is given and the rival cache never passed it. A striped supercar in silver would have read as a rival
in an odd colour, which is the exact failure this feature had to avoid.

MEASURED, AND THE PAINT IS THE MEASUREMENT. Not "was it handed the right palette" - what the sprite
actually came out as, read off its own pixels as saturation, which is how far a colour sits from grey:

    body        as traffic   as a rival
    STALLION      0.134        0.634
    MATADOR       0.162        0.532
    CREST         0.113        0.533

A four-fold difference. That is the thing the ruling said had to be right - "the only thing that says
so is its COLOUR and its BEHAVIOUR" - stated as a number rather than as a screenshot.

VERY VERY RARE, WRITTEN AS ITS OWN ROLL. The owner said it twice, which is the specification, and this
fragment ruled that it "should be written as such rather than folded into the same roll". So the gate
sits BEFORE the type table in both spawners rather than as a slice of it: one number a reader can find.
Sampled over 40,000 spawns it comes to 230 - about one car in 174, against the old rogue's one in 36.

BOTH SPAWNERS, WHICH THE CODE ITSELF WARNS ABOUT. There are two type tables - the main one and the one
for cars coming up behind - and a note above the second says a type added to one and not the other
appears in half the traffic and nowhere else. The gate is in both.

NOT THE FORMULA, and the check asserts its absence rather than assuming it: no formula body reaches
the traffic pool. The owner excluded it by name and RLG-052 records why - it has no indicators,
because it has no business on a public road.

AND THEY INHERIT THE RAISED SPEEDER CHANCE, which is the sentence that could not be built until the
personalities existed. They are in `SPORTY`, so a supercar's driver is a Speeder about 28% of the time
against an ordinary body's 10%. It is a statement about the person who chose to drive one.

A SUPERCAR IN TRAFFIC GETS THE SUPERCAR'S STATS (RLG-042). Their ceilings are 0.90 to 0.95 of top
speed - the highest on the road - and they are slow because the person driving one is going to work,
not because the car was made slower for being traffic. A Civilian in a STALLION does 60 to 80 like
everybody else.

NO REGRESSION. drive-test 27 of 27, face-test finds a face for all 32 vehicles including the three new
ones, and traffic-test's corridor guarantee read 0.308 - inside RLG-037's known one-in-three flake,
which was measured on both arms when the personalities landed.

STILL OPEN, AND NOW THE ONLY THING LEFT IN THIS RULING: nothing. The three personalities are built, the
rare supercars are built, and the display question is answered. What follows from it belongs
elsewhere - the owner has since asked for race OPPONENTS to have a chance of stripes, which is the
same class signal seen from the other end and is its own ruling.

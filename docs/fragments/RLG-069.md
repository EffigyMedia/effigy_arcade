### RLG-069 — The gate on a four-speed shifter still holds five and six
- **RULED 2026-08-29T09:42:02-04:00** — The owner reports, from the device: the manual shifter's gate still carries a fifth and a sixth
position in a car that has four gears. The extra gates are not drawn, but they are still there - the
element is the size of a six-speed gate whatever the car has, so a four-speed shifter is a
six-speed's frame with two thirds of it in use.

Two things to fix, and the second is the one that will be missed. Remove the positions a car does not
have, AND resize the element so it is strictly the gates the car has. A four-speed gate that keeps a
six-speed's footprint leaves the thumb reaching past the top of the pattern for nothing, and on a
phone the size of a control IS the control.

`gears` on the body is the number, and `BODY[key].gears || 6` is how the rest of the engine asks for
it - so nothing has to be invented. The classes to check are the four-speeds (the production and
utility cars, and the muscle car), the five-speeds (roadster, tuner, cruiser) and the six-speeds.
The formula cars use paddles rather than a gate, so they are outside this.

Recorded rather than fixed, at the owner's request.
- **RULED 2026-08-30T10:10:07-04:00** — THE GATE IS THE GEARS THE CAR HAS. Built to the fragment's own description, and the second half was
indeed the one that would have been missed - but not for the reason recorded. Some of the drawing had
already been done: `gears4` and `gears5` classes existed and narrowed the plate. What had not been
done was the part that matters when a thumb is on it.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG, SEEN BY LOOKING AT IT. `tools/gate-shot.py` photographs the plate for a car
of each gear count, and it showed three faults at once that no amount of reading the stylesheet had
turned up:

  1. A FIVE-SPEED WAS DRAWN EXACTLY LIKE A SIX-SPEED. `body.gears5 #gate::after` set the same
     three-rail shadow as the default - a copy, not an override - so the rule did nothing at all.
  2. AND ON A WIDER PLATE THAN THE SIX-SPEED'S. 86 pixels for six gears, 92 for five, 74 for four:
     three hand-set numbers that followed from nothing, one of which made the smaller gearbox the
     bigger control.
  3. THE CROSS RAIL ENDED IN THE MIDDLE OF THE LAST RAIL on both narrowed plates, because it was
     shortened by its own hand-set number rather than by where the rails are.

THE PLATE IS NOW THE GATE IT HOLDS: the last rail's knob position plus the same padding it has on
the left. 54 + 28 + 8 for three rails, 31 + 28 + 8 for two, and the cross rail ends at the outer edge
of the last rail - which comes out as the same inset on both plates, because both plates are their
last rail plus the same padding. A five-speed and a six-speed are the same size, which is right: a
rail carries two gears and they have the same three rails.

AND THE HALF THAT WAS PURELY BEHAVIOURAL. `shiftStep` clamped the knob to `RAIL_X.length - 1`, which
is three whatever you are driving. A four-speed could be walked onto the third rail, and
`gearFactor` returns zero past the end of the ratio table - so the car quietly stopped pulling. It
clamps to the car now. A five-speed keeps its third rail, because it has one, and the slot below
fifth reads NEUTRAL: that is where reverse lives in a road car, and this game has no reverse.

ONE MORE, FOUND WHILE BUILDING IT: the knob kept its position across a change of car, so leaving a
six-speed in sixth and taking out a four-speed left it standing on a rail that car does not have. It
comes back to first, which is where a car you have just got into is.

THE CHECK WALKS THE GATE WITH THE GAME'S OWN `shiftStep`, through a new `API.shift`. A harness that
reimplemented the H-pattern would prove only that the harness agrees with itself. It asserts both
directions - no car reaches a gear it does not have, and no car LOSES one - because a fix that
clamped too hard would have passed the first half alone.

FALSIFIED IN BOTH HALVES, SEPARATELY, AND THEY CATCH DIFFERENT THINGS. Put the old clamp back and the
four-speed reaches the third rail: the gear check still passes, because the slot filter reports
neutral there, and only the RAIL check fails. Put the old slot table back and the five-speeds reach a
sixth gear. Neither check would have caught the other's defect.

AND A HARNESS HAS TO BE A PHONE. The first run of the capture reported a plate 0 by 0 pixels for every
car and no errors: the shell adds `no-touch` to the body when the device reports no touch, and the
whole thumb cluster is `display:none` under it. A desktop context measures nothing and says so
cheerfully. Both new harnesses take a touch context, and this is the first thing to check in any
future harness that measures a control rather than the road.
- **RULED 2026-08-30T10:26:37-04:00** — THE OWNER LOOKED AT THE GATE AND SENT THREE THINGS BACK, 2026-08-30: "Those gear selectors aren't
right. The 4-speed needs to be centered in its housing. We need to make sure that the 5-speed only
shows the 5 positions. I also want production and utility vehicles to have a black leather or plastic
shifter with white text on it."

CENTRING WAS ONE NUMBER, AND IT WAS WRONG ON EVERY PLATE rather than on the four-speed alone. The
rails began at 13 while the plate was built from the knob's travel, so every gate sat left of centre
with the spare width piled on the right. The knob was 4 pixels out too, and that is the same fault
seen from the other side: the knob is 28 across and a rail is 10, so a rail centred under its knob
starts 9 to the right of it, and the knob positions were already 8, 31 and 54. Rails at 17. The cross
rail then ends the same distance from each edge on both plates, because both plates are built the
same way - which is the sign that the geometry is now a rule rather than three numbers.

FIVE POSITIONS MEANT REMOVING THE SIXTH, NOT LABELLING IT. The previous build drew six slots and let
the sixth read NEUTRAL, which is a gearbox fact dressed up as a picture: the player sees a slot and
finds nothing in it. A five-speed's third rail runs from fifth down to the cross rail and stops. The
rails are box-shadow copies of one box, so they are all the same height by construction - a stub of a
different height cannot be one of those copies, and it is its own element.

AND THE KNOB WILL NOT GO DOWN IT. Drawing five positions while the gate still HOLDS six is the same
defect with the picture corrected, and the picture is what the player steers by. The move is refused
when there is no slot to arrive at. Falsified: allow the move again and the knob lands at y 62 where
the check requires the middle at 33.

THE BLACK KNOB IS SIX BODIES. The saloon, the cab, the coupe, the pickup, the van and the lorry - the
fleet's road cars, which are the same bodies that fill the traffic. The sports, super and formula
classes keep the polished ball, because that is what a car chosen for fun has.

THE POLICE CARS ARE NOT IN IT, and that is a decision rather than an oversight. A cruiser is a
production saloon underneath, and on the owner's words it could go either way; this game has always
treated the police as a class of their own, with their own liveries, their own siren and their own
place in the ladder, so they keep the sports knob until the owner says otherwise. It is one entry in
`WORK_BODIES` if they want it.

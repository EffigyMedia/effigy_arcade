### RLG-069 — The gate on a four-speed shifter still holds five and six
- **RULED 2026-08-29T09:42:02-04:00** — The owner reports, from the device: the manual shifter's gate still carries a fifth and a sixth
position in a car that has four gears. The extra gates are not drawn, but they are still there - the
element is the size of a six-speed gate whatever the car has, so a four-speed shifter is a
six-speed's frame with two thirds of it in use.

Two things to fix, and the second is the one that will be missed. Remove the positions a car does not
have, AND resize the element so it is strictly the gates the car has. A four-speed gate that keeps a
six-speed's footprint leaves the thumb reaching past the top of the pattern for nothing, and on a
phone the size of a control IS the control.

`gears` on the body is the number, and `BODY[key].gears || 6` is how the rest of the engine asks for
it - so nothing has to be invented. The classes to check are the four-speeds (the production and
utility cars, and the muscle car), the five-speeds (roadster, tuner, cruiser) and the six-speeds.
The formula cars use paddles rather than a gate, so they are outside this.

Recorded rather than fixed, at the owner's request.

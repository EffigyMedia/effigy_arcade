### RLG-077 — The title menu shows the wrong tail lights
- **RULED 2026-08-29T10:33:25-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-29: the title menu shows the wrong tail lights on the car, and it may be that the
title is not using the right renderer. Recorded, not started, at the owner's request.

The owner's guess is worth keeping because it names the likeliest mechanism. The title's attract card
is drawn by the LAUNCHER's `draw` map, which is a small hand-drawn illustration of each cabinet -
not the engine's sprite painters. So a car on the title is a second drawing of a car, and it was
made before RLG-053 turned every lamp into a declaration that the sprite bakes unlit and re-runs lit.
A second drawing cannot drift back into line on its own: it says whatever it was drawn to say.

That gives two candidate fixes, and they are quite different sizes:

  REDRAW THE CARD to match what the fleet now looks like. Cheap, and it goes stale again the next
  time a lamp moves, which is the fault that produced this report.

  DRAW THE CARD FROM THE ENGINE'S OWN SPRITES, the way the garage and the fleet sheet do. Then a lamp
  is described once and every screen agrees for ever. The launcher is deliberately kept ignorant of
  any machine's internals (see the architecture note in CLAUDE.md), so this needs a seam rather than
  a reach-in: the machine hands the launcher a picture, rather than the launcher knowing how to paint
  a car.

The second is the one that matches every other decision in RLG-053, and it is the reason this
fragment says which renderer rather than which pixels.

To confirm before building: check whether the wrong lamps are on the LAUNCHER's attract card, on the
game's own title screen, or on both - they are different code paths, and the report says "title
menu", which could be either.

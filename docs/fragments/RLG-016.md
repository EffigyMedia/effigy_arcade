### RLG-016 — Interstate: the verge costs speed, not health
- **RULED 2026-08-28T13:39:48-04:00** — - **Owner report:** going off the side of the road should not damage the car, only slow it down.

- **What was happening.** The verge itself never cost health - the concrete barrier beyond it did.
  Past 1.15 lane units the car took `hurt(9, barrier)` on a 0.5s cooldown, so **sliding along a wall
  cost health repeatedly**, and running wide was as expensive as hitting a truck.

- **The ruling behind the change.** Damage is for hitting SOMETHING. A wall you are sliding along is
  not something you hit; it is somewhere you should not be. And in a game about distance against a
  clock, **speed is the currency** - taking it away is already a complete punishment.

- **So the barrier scrubs speed continuously rather than hitting once.** `spd *= 1 - dt*1.9` costs
  more the longer you stay against it, which is what dragging along a wall should feel like, and it
  no longer needs an invulnerability window to stop it firing every frame. The shake is raised so the
  contact still reads.

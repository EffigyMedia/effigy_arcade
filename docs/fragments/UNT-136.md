### UNT-136 - A place can be an event: a stated length and an authored profile

- Routing class: `feature` -> **claude-sonnet-5**, effort **medium**. Bias 1. Implementation to a spec that already answers the design questions. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened for: [[RLG-112]]
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-09-01T09:26:52-04:00** - A place can now be an EVENT rather than a place: it states its own length and, separately, its own shape. That is the mechanism RLG-112 said to build once for the bridge and the tunnel together rather than scripting one road, and the tunnel had left the debt here by being built first without it. TWO FIELDS ON PURPOSE - a tunnel takes the length and not the shape, because the owner has already capped a bore's form and one combined field would have overruled that ruling. Giving the tunnel the length exposed a real defect: a bore ran six and a half to twelve miles, two and a half to four and a half minutes underground against a sixty-second clock, so it could outlast the run it appeared in. AN AUTHORED PROFILE STATES A SLOPE AND NOT A GRADE, which is the whole design - the integration accumulates grade into slope, so a stated grade of zero holds whatever slope the road arrived with, measured at a constant +0.017 continuing to the horizon. BRIDGE is on the board as the first event, and it became a passage by stating one field, which is RLG-140 paying for itself one version on. Green on event-test, tunnel-test, drive-test and smoke-test, with both ways of getting the profile wrong watched going red. The water, the towers and the deck are not built.

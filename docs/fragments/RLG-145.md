### RLG-145 — Device report, 2026-09-01: eight items across six places
- **RULED 2026-09-01T15:54:41-04:00** — RULED 2026-09-01 - Owner, from the device, after play. Eight items, recorded together because
they arrived as one report and none of them has been started. A screenshot accompanied item 4.

1. TUNDRA IS WHITE BEFORE ANYTHING FALLS, AND ONLY THE TARMAC MOVES. "The ground and far
   ground in tundra should be white as if covered in snow. Only the tarmac should increment
   and decrement snow (though keep in mind that the tarmac has a capped minimum baseline of
   .247 I believe it was)."

   THIS IS A CHANGE TO WHAT `snowFloor` MEANS, not a new feature. [[RLG-109]] derives a floor
   from the instance temperature and accumulation builds from it; the owner is saying the
   GROUND and the FAR GROUND are simply white in a tundra, at all times, and that the only
   surface with a moving snow level is the road. The 0.247 the owner remembers is the tundra's
   derived floor - check it rather than trusting either memory. `groundTone` and `groundBase`
   are the two callers and they must not disagree, which is the whole reason `groundBase`
   exists.

2. THE COASTAL SKYLINE ISLANDS ARE MASKED WRONG AT THE SHORELINE. "It might just be simpler to
   remove them." The clip added in v0.11.13 cuts the skyline at the road's vanishing point,
   which is where the shore reaches the horizon - but the shore below that point is NOT at the
   vanishing point, so the mask and the water disagree along the join. The owner offers
   removal; `skyForm:'none'` is one word and loses the distant headlands. Take the owner's
   simpler option unless the mask can be made to follow the shoreline itself.

3. THE COAST SHOULD BARELY RISE AND FALL. "We should minimize the verticality significantly in
   coastal because when we go up and down in this biome the ocean follows and that's not how
   that works." THE OCEAN FOLLOWING IS THE REAL FAULT and the verticality only exposes it: the
   sea is drawn from the road's own line, so a crest lifts the water with it. Reducing `hill`
   is the cheap half and the owner has asked for it. The honest half is the same fix
   [[RLG-143]] needs for the bridge - water drawn at the LAND's level rather than the road's -
   and a coast is the second customer for it. Do the cheap half now and note the rest.

4. THE TUNNEL WALLS ARE DRAWN OVER THE VEHICLES. The screenshot shows traffic inside the bore
   with the wall and floor painted across them. THE CAUSE IS THE DRAW ORDER AND IT IS PLAIN:
   `draw()` calls `drawRoad()`, which paints the road AND EMITS THE SPRITES, and then calls
   `drawBore()`. So the bore paints over every car the road pass already drew. [[RLG-105]]
   chose that order deliberately - "IT IS DRAWN UNDER THE ROAD" was about the tarmac, and the
   sprites were not considered. The fix is not simply to move `drawBore` earlier: the bore
   must be under the CARS and over the SKY, so it belongs between the far field and the road
   pass, or the cars have to be emitted after it.

5. AND BOTH EVENTS TURN TOO HARD. "When the bridge does hard turns it looks odd so let's
   minimize the maximum turn rate the bridge can perform. The turning within the tunnel kind
   of has the same odd effect so maybe reduce its turn rate as well." A bridge is 0.08 on
   `bend` and a tunnel 0.10, which are already the two lowest on the board - so either the cap
   is not being applied inside an event, or the ordinary generator's own floor is what shows.
   MEASURE `roadRoughness` INSIDE A CROSSING before changing a number: a value that is already
   the lowest on the board and still looks wrong is a sign the number is not the one in force.

6. THE CITY NEEDS MORE LIT WINDOWS. "The illuminated windows in the skyline buildings of the
   city biome need to have a higher quantity." [[RLG-095]] rolls each window's habit - its
   phase, cycle and duty - so the lever is the duty and the 0.42 placement chance in
   `skylinePlanFor`, not a brightness. Raising the duty lights more of them at any moment
   without making the city switch as one.

7. THE FARMLAND YARDS ARE TOO DENSE. "The houses and barns in the farmland biome need to be
   more sparse." One number: the FARMLAND scenery spec's `density`, currently 0.30.

8. AND THE FIELDS SHOULD ALTERNATE WITH PASTURE. "Can we also alternate the cornfields with
   pastures? In these pastures should either be horses or cows never at the same time." THE
   ALTERNATION IS THE `crop` MECHANISM ALREADY - a place names the spec its rolled side draws,
   so a second spec and a per-STRETCH choice between them is the shape. NEVER AT THE SAME TIME
   is the part to get right: the choice of beast must be made once for a stretch of pasture
   rather than per animal, or a field holds both. That is the same rule the sea's side and the
   crop's side already follow - one roll, read by many places.

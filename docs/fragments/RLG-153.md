### RLG-153 — The tunnel looks terrible: static walls, wrong mouth relief, jarring transitions, and it may be cut
- **RULED 2026-09-01T21:00:23-04:00** — Owner, 2026-09-01, from the device: "The tunnel looks terrible. I am now convinced that the
shapes of the entrances and exits as far as their relief are wrong for both the tunnel and the
bridge. The bridge at least looks good apart from that, the tunnel does not - the main offender
is that the walls and ceiling of the tunnel are static. They don't move. They should obviously
be moving based on your speed. The way biomes transition into the tunnel and out of the tunnel
is also very jarring. If we can't get the tunnel looking as good as the rest of the biomes, I'm
fine just cutting it from the game, but let's give it a few more tries to get right."

FOUR THINGS, AND THE LAST ONE IS THE ONE TO KEEP IN VIEW. The tunnel is on probation. It is the
only place on the board that has been offered up for deletion, and the standard it has to reach
is stated: as good as the rest of the biomes. Every attempt below is spent against that.

    1. THE WALLS AND THE CEILING ARE STATIC, and the owner calls this the main offender. It is
       one line and it is a real defect rather than a matter of taste. `borePoints` samples the
       tube at `z0 + t*t*reach`, and once you are inside, `z0` is the CAMERA - so every rib,
       joint and lamp sits at a fixed distance ahead of you for ever. The shape was correct and
       the picture never changed: a painted backdrop with a road running into it.

       MEASURED: the whole ceiling band differed by 2.2 per cent between two frames 180ms apart
       at seven tenths of top speed, and that much is the tube swinging on a bend rather than
       anything going past.

       BUILT at 0.11.24. The joints, the wall panels and the lamps are drawn at fixed multiples
       of `BORE.ring` in WORLD units, so they come at you and pass overhead. The surfaces keep
       the camera-relative walk, because a wall and a vault are continuous and their silhouette
       is the same however it is sampled. Same measurement afterwards: 20.9 per cent, with a
       parked control still at zero.

    2. THE MOUTH RELIEF IS WRONG FOR BOTH THE TUNNEL AND THE BRIDGE. This is [[RLG-148]],
       already recorded from an earlier and more tentative report - the owner said then that
       they had not been fully paying attention. They are now convinced, and about both places
       rather than one. It stops being a measurement to schedule and becomes the second thing
       to fix.

    3. THE TRANSITIONS IN AND OUT ARE JARRING. Not yet diagnosed and it must not be guessed at.
       Three things cross at a tunnel's mouth and they do not share a clock: `placeDark` ramps
       over 18 segments going in and 36 coming out, the biome colour blend runs over
       `BIOME_BAND` at 18, and the weather crosses over `WEATHER_BAND` at 72. A fourth, the
       bore's own extent, is not a ramp at all - it is solid or absent ([[RLG-144]]). Any two of
       those disagreeing is a candidate, and so is the portal, which is a rock wall drawn across
       the road at the boundary.

    4. AND IT MAY BE CUT. If it cannot be brought up to the rest of the board the owner is
       content to lose it. That is not a threat to work around - it is a budget. A later session
       should read it as permission to stop rather than as a reason to keep spending.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT TOUCH. The bore's proportions, its lighting and its colour were all settled
against the owner's own reference photographs in RLG-105 and are not in question here. The
owner says the bridge looks good apart from its mouth, so nothing else about the bridge is
either.

### RLG-064 — Traffic never de-spawns ahead, so stopping empties the road
- **RULED 2026-08-29T04:13:26-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-29, from the device:**

> "Also when I stop traffic isn't spawning behind me."

**FOUND AND FIXED in v0.9.16, and the spawner was innocent.** `spawnBehind` is called correctly:
two seconds under the flow speed starts a timer, and the further below the flow you are the more
often it fires. Every part of that works. It is gated on `traffic.length < 26`, **and the array
could never get back under 26.**

**There was no forward cull at all.** Traffic was removed only once it had fallen 34,000 units
BEHIND the player. Anything quicker than the player drove away up the road and stayed in the array
for the rest of the run.

**Measured at a standstill, before the fix:**

| after | total | ahead | behind | past the draw distance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.5s | 30 | 28 | 2 | 12 |
| 5s | 30 | 30 | 0 | 19 |
| 10s | 30 | 30 | 0 | 29 |
| 15s | 30 | 30 | 0 | **30** |

**An empty road, thirty invisible cars, and a simulation still paying for all of them every frame.**
That is also a large part of why [[RLG-041]]'s instrument reported 68-72% of every vehicle-frame as
`unbucketed`: most of the traffic was outside the drawn road and had been for minutes.

**The fix is one line in the cull, at `pos + 64000`.** The wave spawner reaches to `pos + 62000`, so
anything past that is beyond the furthest road this run has built, and it is not coming back without
being re-spawned in front of the player anyway.

**Measured after, same test:** the array settles at 16-21 with two to four cars behind the player at
any moment, rather than 30 and none.

**Guarded, and the guard is not the array size.** `traffic-test` now tags every car that exists at
the moment of stopping and asks whether any UNTAGGED car has since arrived behind the player. A car
that was already behind proves nothing, and the count alone proves nothing - only a new arrival
does. It reads `2 new cars came up behind over 20s`.

**Worth stating because it is the general shape:** the defect was in the CULL and the symptom was in
the SPAWNER, two functions apart, and every part of the spawner read correctly. A population with an
entry rule and no exit rule fills up, and the first thing to break is whatever is gated on how full
it is.

### RLG-087 — The garage car sits in one box, sized to the tallest car
- **RULED 2026-08-30T19:56:13-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-30: set the car render in the garage to the same static height, using the tallest car as the reference, so that switching cars does not collapse and expand the layout by a small amount. The owner's word for the current behaviour is annoying, and the size of the movement is the point - a large change would read as a transition, and a small one reads as the page failing to hold still.

THE BOX IS RESERVED, NOT THE CAR. The car keeps its own proportions and its own size; what stops moving is the space it is drawn in. A lorry is much taller than a roadster and forcing them to the same drawn height would make the roadster wrong rather than the layout right.

MEASURE THE FLEET RATHER THAN NAMING THE LORRY. The reference is the tallest sprite box any selectable car has, taken from the same table the sprites are built from, so a car added later cannot silently overflow the reservation or leave a gap under it. A hard-coded number here is the same mistake as the pedal box's hard-coded 58, which sat in a different file from the two numbers that decided it.

AND THE CAR SITS ON THE FLOOR OF THAT BOX RATHER THAN IN THE MIDDLE OF IT. Cars stand on the ground. Centring a roadster in a lorry's box leaves it floating with a gap underneath, which trades one wrong layout for another.

RELATED, NOT THE SAME: RLG-086 made the garage answer a tap in about four milliseconds instead of about a quarter of a second. That was work per tap; this is layout per car, and the second is visible now that the first has stopped hiding it.

- **CLOSED 2026-08-30T10:10:08-04:00** - The gate now holds the gears the car has and the plate is the size of the gate it holds. Three faults were visible in the first capture and none of them in the stylesheet: a five-speed drawn exactly like a six-speed because its rule was a copy of the default, on a plate wider than a six-speed's, with the cross rail ending in the middle of the last rail. The behavioural half was the clamp - the knob's travel was bounded by the rail table rather than by the car, so a four-speed could be walked onto a third rail where gearFactor returns zero and the car stops pulling. Falsified in both halves separately, and they catch different things. Two new harnesses, both in a touch context, because the shell hides the whole thumb cluster on a device that reports none - the first capture measured a plate zero pixels wide and reported no errors.

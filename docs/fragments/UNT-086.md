- **CLOSED 2026-08-30T23:19:47-04:00** - The scenery's approach obeys perspective exactly, so nothing about the road changed. Following one tree from 30,000 units in to 12,500 on a flattened road, apparent width times distance holds at 275,141 with 0.00 per cent drift, and frame-to-frame growth never exceeds 2 per cent. scenery-test keeps that as a regression check and a deliberate distance-dependent scale error drifts it 14.7 per cent. TWO EARLIER MEASUREMENTS WERE THE INSTRUMENT, NOT THE GAME: one waited 1.4s of a 3s count-in so the car was stationary through most of it, and one read the road position after the frame while the width was recorded during it, which put a couple of per cent of skew in and read as drift. The position is stamped with the width now, and the road is flattened first because near the horizon an object moves mostly because the terrain under it does. WHAT IS LEFT IS TASTE: an object is invisible until about 30,000 units and not solid until about 22,000, which is the stretch where it moves most slowly, so it arrives as a smudge that barely moves and turns solid just as it starts to accelerate past.

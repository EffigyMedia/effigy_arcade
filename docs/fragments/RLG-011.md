### RLG-011 — The wordmarks match the new names: a REDLINE lockup, and HARDPOINT cut from hull plate
- **RULED 2026-08-28T12:23:15-04:00** — - **Quietus needed nothing.** Its wordmark is a blood-smear stencil drawn through the painter's
  `paint` hook, and the rename already moved the word it draws. Checked before changing anything.

- **The two driving titles were set as one line, and it said the wrong thing.** `REDLINE INTERSTATE`
  filled the frame edge to edge and read as a single long name. It is not one. **REDLINE is the
  marque both driving games share** - one engine, one garage, six cars - and INTERSTATE is which of
  the two you are about to drive. A line of eighteen characters says the opposite, and it shrinks the
  half that actually names the game to make room for the half that does not.

  It is a lockup now: the marque small and letterspaced above, on the cool ramp only, and the
  discipline large beneath in the game's own palette. **The discipline is BIGGER than the one-line
  version was**, because it no longer shares the width. A single-word title falls through to the old
  path untouched, which is what a fork bringing its own name gets.

- **HARDPOINT is cut from hull plate, and the letters are a MASK rather than something coloured in.**
  `Arcade.wordmark` lays the shapes into an offscreen canvas and `source-in` clips the material to
  them. The indirection is the technique: the painter's own `paint` hook is stroke-based, which is
  right for a smear and wrong for a material, because **brushed grain, panel seams and rivets have to
  run ACROSS the letters as if the word were cut from one sheet.** Painted per stroke they restart at
  every letter and read as texture-on-letters instead of letters-cut-from-plate.

  The plate is built bottom to top: a rolled-steel gradient lit from above, directional machine grain
  (a random speckle reads as dirt - brushed metal is directional), three panel seams with rivets down
  them falling wherever they fall rather than politely between letters, a lapped horizontal seam,
  shallow scuffs, a rim light on the top edge, and a warm bounce underneath from the engine glow the
  rest of the cabinet is lit by.

- **THE COMPOSITING TRAP, and it cost a wrong render.** `source-in` keeps the source where the
  destination is opaque **and clears the destination everywhere else**. The first attempt left the
  mode on and painted the plate straight into the mask - so every partial fill after the first erased
  what the one before it had kept. The grain loop alone is one `fillRect` per row, and the mark came
  out almost black, showing only the dark shell pass underneath.

  **The mask was never the problem**, which is why it was measured rather than guessed at: a probe
  counted 17 paint calls and 9,216 lit pixels in it. The fix is to build the material whole on its
  own canvas and composite it in **one** draw.

- **Proof.** `pack.sh` all gates; `smoke-test` 6/6; `drive-test` 21/21 after the `road.js` change.
  Both driving titles and the hull mark were rendered and looked at, not assumed.

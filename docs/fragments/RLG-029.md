### RLG-029 — Interstate: verify the rubber banding actually works
- **RULED 2026-08-28T13:51:55-04:00** — - **Owner request:** verify that rubber banding works correctly.

- **This is a measurement, not a change.** Drive a rival field with the harness, sample the gap over
  a run, and see whether a rival that falls behind actually recovers and whether one that gets ahead
  is actually reined in.

- **Do not tune it before measuring it.** The complaint is that it may not work, and a change made on
  a guess cannot afterwards be told apart from one made on a fact.
- **RULED 2026-08-28T16:53:35-04:00** — **MEASURED, no change made.** The band is half broken. The governor works and the tow does not exist.

Measured with `tools/band-test.py` (new, this unit), evidence `[[EVD-004]]`, three paired runs of 25
seconds each. Each arm boots Interstate, starts a single race, and PINS three rivals to a fixed lead
on every tick, so the band value each one sees is a constant rather than a number that drifts
differently in the two arms. Speed is the reading, not the gap to the player: the player's car is
20mph quicker than `AI_TOP`, so the gap to a rival behind grows whatever the band does.

| held at | band value | band on | band off | band on minus off | per-run spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.00 miles behind | +14%, saturated | 179.0mph | 178.7mph | **+0.17%** | -0.02, +0.57, -0.04 |
| 0.10 miles behind | +1.1% | 98.1mph | 110.8mph | -8.91% | -32.67, +28.30, -22.36 |
| 2.00 miles ahead | -14%, saturated | 154.0mph | 167.0mph | **-7.78%** | -7.82, -7.76, -7.77 |

**The tow does nothing at all.** A rival held two miles behind runs the same speed whether the band
is on or off, and it sits at the `AI_TOP` ceiling 92% of the time in BOTH arms.

The cause is one line of ordering. `want` is multiplied by the band, and then the very next line
caps it: `want = Math.min(want, AI_TOP)`. Every rival's `base` is already ABOVE that cap - the
measured headroom is -4.4% for the rival tested, and negative for all three tagged cars - because
`base` is derived from `r.vmax`, which is `MAX_SPD` times a body multiplier of 0.97 to 1.03, while
`AI_TOP` is a flat `MAX_SPD * 180/200`. So `want` is over the ceiling before the band is applied and
still over it after a 14% tow, and the cap discards the whole thing. The tow is not weak. It is
unreachable, and no amount of tuning the 0.11 coefficient or the 0.14 limit will make it reachable.

**The governor works, at about eight of the claimed fourteen points.** It survives the same cap
because it pulls `want` DOWN and the cap only ever pulls down as well. The arithmetic: the tested
rival's `base` is `AI_TOP` x 1.065, band off gives `min(base, AI_TOP)` = `AI_TOP`, band on gives
`base` x 0.86 = `AI_TOP` x 0.916, so -8.4% is predicted and -7.78% is measured. The 0.6 point
residual is acceleration lag and occasional traffic. The three runs agree to within 0.06 points,
which is what a real effect looks like.

**The middle row is not evidence of anything and must not be read as though it were.** A rival held
a tenth of a mile back is inside traffic, its speed is set by `want = min(want, blocking car)`
rather than by the band, and its three runs disagree by sixty points. A +1.1% tow cannot produce
-8.91%. The row is kept in the report because deleting an inconvenient measurement is worse than
labelling it, and because it is the reason `--repeats` exists: the FIRST pair this harness ever ran
reported that tag at -23% from a single run, which read as a finding and was weather.

**Why the measurement is not vacuous.** The control arm rewrites `const band = clamp(...)` to
`const band = 0` in the HTTP server, in memory, touching no file on disk, and the rewrite is
asserted to hit exactly one line - a control that silently patched nothing would report two
identical runs as proof of inertness. That control demonstrably CAN see a difference: it sees the
governor at -7.78% with a spread of 0.06 points. So the +0.17% on the tow is evidence that the tow
does nothing, not evidence that the instrument is blind.

An earlier version of this harness was itself wrong and is worth recording, because its output was
plausible. It ran the two arms as two DIFFERENT races - grid, paints and traffic all come from
`Math.random` - and reported that the tow made a rival 11mph SLOWER. That was two grids, not an
effect. `Math.random` is now seeded from the harness before any game script runs, the same seed in
both arms.

**A second owner question is answered in passing, by measurement rather than by a code read.**
Rivals held two miles ahead and two miles behind were still in `racers` and still being simulated
for the whole 25-second window, in all six runs. They do not de-spawn at two miles. This tests two
miles and says nothing about larger distances.

**Nothing is tuned.** RLG-029 says measure, and the fix is a decision the owner has not been asked
for yet: the tow can only start working if the `AI_TOP` cap stops being applied after the band, and
that changes how fast rivals can ever go. That is a design question, not a bug fix, and it is put to
the owner rather than taken.

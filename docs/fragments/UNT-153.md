- **CLOSED 2026-09-01T21:03:04-04:00** - The inside of a tunnel goes past you. RLG-153 item 1, which the owner called the main offender. borePoints samples the tube at z0 + t*t*reach and once you are inside z0 IS the camera, so every rib, joint and lamp sat at a fixed distance ahead for ever - the shape was right and the picture never changed. The joints, wall panels and lamps hang on RINGS at world positions now, every 1,050 units, with the lamps on every second one. The surfaces keep the camera-relative walk deliberately: a wall and a vault are continuous and only detail can be seen to move. borePointAt is one description of the cross-section rather than two. Measured in the pixels because the sample list was never wrong: the ceiling band went from 2.2 per cent changed between frames at speed to 20.9, with a parked control at zero both ways.

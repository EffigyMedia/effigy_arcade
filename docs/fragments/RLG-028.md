### RLG-028 — Interstate: cars without NOS do not collapse the gauges to the pedals
- **RULED 2026-08-28T13:51:42-04:00** — - **Owner report:** cars without NOS did not have the gauges collapse down to the pedals.

- **What that means.** The HUD reserves room for a nitrous gauge whether or not the chosen car has
  one, so a car without it drives with a hole in its instrument layout instead of the remaining
  gauges closing up.

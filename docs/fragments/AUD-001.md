### AUD-001 — The first audit of this repository: eight findings, none critical
- **RULED 2026-08-30T12:03:30-04:00** — THE FIRST AUDIT OF THIS REPOSITORY, run at 0.9.96 as the last item of the owner's queue. The report
is docs/audits/audit_260830_120400.md and it changed no code, which is the rule: findings, not fixes.

NOTHING CRITICAL, AND THE PRODUCT IS IN GOOD HEALTH. Every gate passes, the build is clean, the
standalone exit route still emits one self-contained file, and the four cabinets boot and run. There
is no TODO, FIXME or HACK marker anywhere in the shipped source.

THE MOST SERIOUS FINDING IS NOT IN THE CODE. Twenty-six rulings the changelog says shipped are still
marked `requested`, and there is no written rule for when a multi-part ruling becomes `built`. That
is not a tidiness complaint: two of the ten items in the owner's own queue today turned out to be
already built, and both were only found out by reading the code. A third of a working day was planned
against a record that was wrong.

AND THE GAP IS A MISSING RULE RATHER THAN LAZINESS, which is why the recommendation is a conversation
before a reconciliation. Several of the twenty-six are legitimately open - RLG-059 shipped nine times
today and still has work in it. Others are finished and simply never flipped. Nothing says which is
which, so the honest default has been to leave them alone.

ONE NEW DEFECT, AND IT IS IN WORK THIS SESSION SHIPPED. `coast-test` fails about one run in four on
two checks together, and the number that names the cause is a 57-pixel step in a shoreline whose
average step is 3.45. The far band under the horizon derives its shoreline from the road's vanishing
point while the drawn slices derive theirs per slice, and on a steeply falling road the two
constructions meet at an angle. Recorded rather than fixed, and the fix wants the capture loop rather
than more reasoning - this would be the third time the coast has been got wrong by thinking about it.

THE LAUNCHER HAD NEVER BEEN AUDITED and it is where two findings came from: three of its own stores
break the documented key shape, and fifteen of its eighteen attract painters are dead code left from
Tiny Arcade's nineteen machines - in the one file where a stray brace silently kills the stylesheet.

WHAT THE AUDIT WAS JUDGED AGAINST IS WORTH RECORDING, because it is not what the process assumes.
This project has no design document of its own: PRIVATEER.md is Hardpoint's, frozen and pre-rename,
and the rest of docs/reference is Tiny Arcade's. The contract is CLAUDE.md plus the fragment store.
That works, because the store is unusually good - but it means there is no single statement of what
any of the four machines is meant to BE, and it is why the report says so rather than pretending to a
design-conformance pass it could not do.

AND THE COVERAGE STATEMENT IS HONEST ABOUT ITS OWN LIMITS. Quietus and Hardpoint were audited at
boot-and-paint level only; the driving games got the depth, because that is where the day's work was
and where the engine is shared. Every figure came from a headless desktop browser. Security, privacy
and dependencies were checked at the level this product actually has - no keys, no third-party
runtime code, no network call at launch, and nothing leaving the device.

AUD-002 WAS MOSTLY A DEFECT IN THE CHECK, AND THE AUDIT SAID OTHERWISE. It reported a 57-pixel step
in the shoreline as a fault in the game. Going to fix it produced a picture of the frame the check
had failed on, and the shoreline in it is a clean straight diagonal. The finding was wrong, and it
was wrong because the metric assumed something about the edge's ANGLE.

FOUR FAULTS IN THE CHECK, EACH FOUND BY LOOKING AT WHAT IT MEASURED.

  1. THE LARGEST STEP BETWEEN NEIGHBOURING ROWS is only meaningful while the edge is steeper than 45
     degrees. Where the shoreline runs shallow - which is exactly what a steeply falling road does to
     it - one row spans a hundred pixels of x on a perfectly straight edge. That produced the 57, and
     a 121 on a later run.
  2. IT COMPARED ROWS THAT DO NOT TOUCH. Rows where the edge has left the frame were dropped, and the
     remaining ones were treated as neighbours - so an edge going off the right of the screen and
     returning thirty rows later read as a 119-pixel kink.
  3. A SECOND DIFFERENCE is large on any shallow edge and on a real bend, which a coast road has by
     design.
  4. A LOCAL STRAIGHT-LINE FIT handles all of that and still cannot tell a staircase from a CREST.
     A brow hides the slices between two shorelines, so the water's edge legitimately steps once
     where the road crests - and the ground never shows it, because the ground fills the full width
     and only the sea has a lateral edge to show it with.

WHAT THE TEST IS NOW, stated without reference to angle: a staircase is an edge whose steps are
mostly nothing and occasionally a jump. So the measurement is against the edge's OWN median step, and
what it counts is outliers. A smooth edge at any angle has near-constant steps and no outliers; a
crest has one; a staircase has one every few rows for ever. Measured: 0 outliers in five consecutive
runs with the quad, and 6 to 8 with the rectangle put back.

AND ITS HONEST LIMIT: it catches the rectangle three times in four rather than four. On a frame where
the shoreline is shallow the median step rises to 4 and the staircase's own steps of 10 no longer
stand out against it. That is a MISS rather than a false alarm, which is the right way round for a
gate somebody has to trust - the old check failed one run in four on correct pictures, which is how a
check gets switched off.

ONE REAL FAULT WAS FOUND ON THE WAY, and it was not the one the audit named. The sea's fill carries
its edge down to the bottom of the screen so the nearest slice ends on the shoreline's own angle
rather than dropping vertically, and that angle came from the slice's own two ends. A slice at the
far end of the draw is a third of a pixel tall, so dividing by its height turned a rounding error
into a line across the frame - wherever a nearer slice then failed to paint over the tail. A slice
must be taller than two pixels to have an angle worth reading.

THE GENERAL SHAPE IS WORTH THE WORDS. This is the fifth instrument in this project to be noisier than
the effect it was pointed at, after the frame-rate probe, the cull count, the biome sweep and the
live-frame lamp diff. The pattern is now unmistakable: every one of them measured a DOWNSTREAM
quantity and assumed something about the thing upstream. The ones that worked measured the thing that
changed.

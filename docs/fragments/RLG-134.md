### RLG-134 — The centre-screen messages block the road ahead
- **RULED 2026-08-31T21:04:58-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-08-31, after playing through all the tournaments: "the UI elements that pop up in the
dead center of the screen block my view of the upcoming road, so I think we should move that to just
under the other information that's below the rearview mirror."

THIS ANSWERS THE QUESTION [[RLG-082]] WAS WAITING ON. That fragment recorded the owner asking for
"the anchored relative spacing of UI elements" and could not be started, because which elements on
which screen was not known and the note said it must be asked with a capture rather than guessed.
This is the answer, arrived at by playing: it is the transient messages, on the driving screen, and
they are in the wrong PLACE rather than the wrong size.

THERE ARE TWO SEPARATE MECHANISMS PRINTING IN THE MIDDLE OF THE SCREEN, and both have to move or half
the messages will still be over the road.

  THE BANNER, `#warn`, is a DOM element and it is styled at `top:38%` in both `interstate.html` and
  `motorsport.html`. `flashWarn()` writes to it. This is what says CHECKPOINT, ROADBLOCK, BUSTED,
  WRONG WAY and the biome name as you arrive in a new place.

  THE FLOATING LABELS are canvas text in the `fx` array, pushed at `x:W/2` with `y` between `H*0.50`
  and `H*0.62`, and they drift upward. PIT MANOEUVRE, THREADED THE GAP, CRUISER DOWN and the
  checkpoint time award are these. There are four push sites in `road.js`.

WHERE THEY SHOULD GO IS ALREADY A COMPUTED POSITION, which makes this cheaper than it looks. The
information row under the mirror is `.toprow`, and it is placed at

    padding-top: calc(var(--mirror-top,6px) + var(--mirror-h,44px) + 8px * var(--ark-ui,1))

so the shell already publishes where the bottom of the mirror is. The messages should hang off the
same two variables rather than off a new percentage - a percentage is what put them at 38% in the
first place, and it is why they land on the road on one phone and not on another.

FOUR THINGS THAT WILL BITE:

  A MACHINE MUST NEVER DEFINE `--stage-h` OR `--safe-top`. `--mirror-h` and `--mirror-top` are the
  right variables to read and neither of those two may be declared in a cabinet's own `:root`.

  THE CANVAS LABELS DO NOT SEE CSS. `fx` is drawn into the canvas, so it cannot read `--mirror-h`
  directly; the pixel row has to be passed in or computed the same way the mirror's own geometry is.
  The two mechanisms must land in the same band or the move will look like a bug.

  `#warn` IS FULL-WIDTH AND `pointer-events:none`, and the comment above it records why: while a
  warning was on screen it silently swallowed every tap in its band. Whatever it becomes must keep
  that, and moving it into the busier top of the screen makes it matter more, not less.

  MOTORSPORT HIDES IT IN THE PITS - `body.in-pit #warn{display:none!important}`. That must survive.

AND THE READABILITY TRADE SHOULD BE PUT TO THE OWNER RATHER THAN DECIDED HERE. Directly under the
mirror is the busiest part of the screen: the mirror above it, the information row in it, and now
transient messages as well. The messages are currently large - `clamp(20px,6.4vw,30px)` - because
they are alone in the middle of the frame, and they will almost certainly need to be smaller where
they are going. That is a judgment on a device.

`interstate.html` and `motorsport.html` BOTH CARRY THE RULE and both must change. `quietus` and
`hardpoint` have no `#warn`.

NOT STARTED.

### RLG-040 — Traffic merges only a fraction of a lane, and does not finish the move
- **RULED 2026-08-28T17:33:05-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-28:** "when traffic merges, they don't go fully to a new lane. It's like a fraction
of a lane. We need to make sure they fully merge a new lane, and that when we make the roadway
larger that persists."

**The same fault is measured from the rivals' side in [[RLG-033]] part 1, evidence [[EVD-007]]:**
rivals spend about 60% of their time off a lane centre with the lateral term on, against 0% with it
removed. They are not overtaking and they are not in a lane either.

**The shape of it.** A lane change is written as a per-frame lateral RATE that lasts exactly as long
as something is in front of the car. When the pressure goes, the car is pulled back toward the
centre of the lane index it started in - and nothing ever updates that index. So a car leans out far
enough to unblock its own scan, stops leaning, and returns to where it began. There is no target
lane and no notion of the move being finished.

**What is wanted:** a lane change has a TARGET lane, the car commits to it, and it is finished -
either completed or abandoned as a decision, not left half done by a decaying pressure.

**The second half of the ruling is a constraint on the fix, not a separate job.** [[RLG-024]] widens
the road, and the owner requires that full merging still holds afterwards. That rules out any fix
expressed in absolute lateral distance or in a hard-coded four-lane table: it has to be expressed in
LANES. `LANE_X` and `LANES` already exist and the fix must go through them.

Traffic and rivals are two code paths and this is one idea. They may be two units; they may not be
two designs.

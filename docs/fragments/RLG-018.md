### RLG-018 — Interstate: the damage plume no longer owns the frame
- **RULED 2026-08-28T13:40:42-04:00** — - **Owner report:** the damage effects hide the road ahead. Make them smaller.

- **How they got that big, and it was not carelessness.** The comment above the emitter records the
  reasoning: sixteen puffs read as a wisp rather than a plume, so the count went up roughly fourfold
  to make the column hold together as one shape while it rises. That was the right fix for the
  problem in front of it. **It just did not stop at the point where the column became the view.**

- **Why it matters more than it looks.** At high damage the plume filled the middle of the frame and
  hid the traffic you were about to hit. **A game that punishes damage by taking the road away turns
  one mistake into all of them** - you cannot recover from a bad state if the bad state is what stops
  you seeing.

- **What changed.** Shorter, narrower, thinner, and fewer, for smoke, flame licks and embers alike -
  the rise roughly halved, since it was climbing past the horizon line. **What says wrecked car is
  the SOURCE and the colour, not the acreage**, so it still reads instantly.

- **Not changed:** where it comes from, the clip to above the roofline, and the fact that the column
  stays vertical while the body leans. Those are the parts that make it read as an engine bay you
  are sitting behind, and they were never the problem.

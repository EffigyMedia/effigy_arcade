### RLG-146 — The desert skyline is brown rust, and it carries more detail
- **RULED 2026-09-01T16:54:35-04:00** — Owner, 2026-09-01, mid-session: "The skyline steps in the desert need to be brown/rust
colored with higher detail."

THE COLOUR IS NOT A PAINTER FAULT AND IT IS ONE MISSING FIELD. `buildSkyline` reads
`B.skyBase` and falls back to `#150c22` when a place does not state one - a dark purple
that was chosen for a night city. DESERT states no `skyBase`, so its mesas are painted in
that purple and hazed toward the sky, which is why they read as slate steps rather than as
rock. CANYON states `SANDSTONE.mid` and looks right for exactly that reason.

THIS IS THE SHAPE [[RLG-104]] ALREADY RULED ON. A place states what its rock is made of, the
way it states its rain, and the painter recognises no names. So the fix is a `skyBase` on
DESERT in a brown rust, not a branch on `B.name === 'DESERT'` anywhere.

AND THE DETAIL IS THE `mesa` ARM OF `paintSkylineShape`. Every other silhouette in that
function got an inside when the shapes were given form - a peak has a sunward face and a snow
line, a tower has a setback and a mast. A mesa has one trapezoid and one talus skirt, which is
the least of any of them. More detail here means more of the same kind of thing: a caprock
band along the top, a stepped bench or two down the flank, and the talus reading as scree
rather than as a single triangle. The lit flank is already an argument to the painter, so the
new marks take their colour from `flank` and stay correct at every hour.

WHAT TO CHECK. `tools/skyline-test.py` and `tools/biome-shot.py --biomes DESERT`. The colour
is the owner's call and a screenshot is the evidence for it.

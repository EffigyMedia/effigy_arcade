- **CLOSED 2026-09-01T15:20:41-04:00** - The two profiles are in and the water is not. THE TUNNEL DOES THE OPPOSITE OF THE BRIDGE and it is the same shape negated, through the same machinery, with nothing new built for it - which is the test of whether the event mechanism was a mechanism. The bridge runs 0 to +42 and back, the tunnel 0 to -24 and back; a bore drops less than a span rises because a bridge clears shipping and a tunnel only gets under the ground. THE MOUTHS MEET, which the fragment named as where this fails: zero slope at an end is not zero height, and the two ramps being equal and opposite makes the integral exactly zero - checked at 0.000000 before any of it was wired. RLG-112 was right to withhold this profile and wait for the ruling rather than guess it, and the tunnel's 0.10 cap stays because it answers a different question. The second accumulator is built. THE WATER IS NOT DRAWN AT IT and the obstacle is a units one worth stating: both accumulators are read through functions that subtract the player's own value, which is correct for the road and wrong for the water - at the car's own position that makes the difference zero, so the sea rose to meet the deck exactly where it should have been furthest below. The rise has to enter the projection as a WORLD height the way the bore's ceiling does, which is the same mistake that cost three builds there. WATER_DROP stands until then. Also recorded, not fixed: RLG-144, the owner asked why the tunnel is transparent - its opacity IS the crossing ramp, so mid-transition the whole bore is drawn at half alpha and the world shows through it.

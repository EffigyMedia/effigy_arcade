### RLG-150 — A place's bend and hill do not reach the first 70,000 units of it, because the road is generated before the place is chosen
- **RULED 2026-09-01T18:29:23-04:00** — FOUND WHILE MEASURING [[RLG-145]] ITEM 5, WHICH IS WHY THAT ITEM SAID TO MEASURE FIRST. The
owner reported that the bridge and the tunnel both turn too hard. A bridge is 0.08 on `bend`
and a tunnel 0.10, the two lowest numbers on the board, and the item's own note said that a
place which is already lowest and still looks wrong is a sign the number is not the one in
force. It is not.

THE ROAD IS GENERATED BEFORE THE PLACE THAT WILL OCCUPY IT IS CHOSEN. Two distances, both read
straight out of the file:

    `rebuildBend()` generates curvature and grade out to `pos + 100000`.

    A new place is decided at `biomeEdge = here + DRAW`, and DRAW is 150 segments of 200
    units, so it begins 30,000 ahead of the camera.

`pushCurve` scales each segment by `shapeAt(...)` AT GENERATION, deliberately - the note there
says a bend that changed magnitude as you approached it would be the road moving under you, and
that is correct. But it means the 70,000 units of road between where the place begins and where
generation has already reached were made under the PREVIOUS place's factor, and nothing ever
revisits them. `pushHill` does the same thing with `hill`.

MEASURED BY DRIVING, NOT BY READING THE TABLE. Sampling a span from a standstill cannot answer
this: `curvatureAt` reads the segment list, which is trimmed behind the camera and only
generated so far ahead, so everything outside that window answers 0 and a straight cannot be
told from a road that does not exist yet. Two earlier attempts were wrong for exactly that
reason. The measurement that works drives the passage at a fixed short look-ahead and buckets
the readings by how far through the passage they were taken. Ordinary farmland driven the same
way is the baseline.

    BRIDGE, span 218,790, wanted 0.23 as bent as farmland all the way through.
    By eighths: 1.29, 1.39, 0.28, 0.27, 0.11, 0.30, 0.09, 0.24.

    TUNNEL, span 180,180, wanted 0.29 as bent all the way through.
    By eighths: 2.24, 0.72, 0.82, 0.55, 0.43, 0.15, 0.35, 0.36.

So the entry to a bridge is about SIX TIMES as bent as it is meant to be and the entry to a
tunnel about EIGHT TIMES, and both settle to roughly the right figure part way along. A
bridge's eighths are 27,349 units each, so its step falls between 54,700 and 82,000 - which is
where 70,000 lands. The tunnel's is less sharp across one sample and a coarse bucket, but its
first eighth is the worst reading of either.

THIS IS NOT A PASSAGE PROBLEM AND THAT IS THE PART THAT NEEDS THE OWNER. Every place on the
board is entered through 70,000 units of the previous place's shape. A passage only makes it
obvious because a bridge and a tunnel are the two calmest things in the game and the contrast
is largest. A mountain entered from a city is the same fault with the sign reversed - its first
70,000 units are far calmer than a mountain should be.

WHICH MEANS FIXING IT CHANGES HOW EVERY TRANSITION FEELS, not just the two the owner named. The
tables were tuned by feel WITH this fault present, so making them true will move everything at
once. That is the owner's call and this fragment does not make it.

THREE WAYS TO FIX IT, AND ONLY ONE LOOKS RIGHT.

    GENERATE LESS FAR AHEAD. One number - `need` in `rebuildBend`. It is wrong: the same walk
    feeds the bend integration, and the far bands need it. The coast's shoreline alone is
    carried about twice the draw distance past the drawn road, so 100,000 is sized for a
    reason and cutting it would break the horizon.

    DECIDE THE PLACE FURTHER AHEAD. Move the decision out to where generation reaches while
    leaving `biomeEdge` at the horizon, so the colour still enters the picture at the horizon
    and travels in, as [[RLG-022]] requires. This separates WHERE THE PLACE IS from WHEN IT WAS
    CHOSEN, and those are two different questions that are currently one variable.

    RESCALE ON PLACEMENT. When a place is placed, walk the segments that fall inside it and
    multiply each by the new factor over the old. Exact, and local - the old factor is
    recoverable because those segments were generated while `biomeFrom` was current. The risk
    is a segment straddling the edge, and the note in `pushCurve` about a bend changing
    magnitude as you approach it: this rescales road that is up to 70,000 units away, which is
    beyond the drawn 30,000, so nothing on screen would change under the player. That needs
    checking rather than assuming.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR WORK ALREADY DONE. [[RLG-145]] item 3 lowered the coast's relief to the
board's floor and measured the water's travel at 12 to 20 pixels. That measurement was taken on
a coast the road had settled into, so the entrance to a coast is worse than the figure recorded
there. The change stands; the number is a floor rather than a ceiling.

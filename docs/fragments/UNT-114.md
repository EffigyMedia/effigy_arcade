### UNT-114 - The bottle is worth opening at the top of the last gear

- Routing class: `feature` -> **claude-sonnet-5**, effort **medium**. Bias 1. Implementation to a spec that already answers the design questions. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-08-31T19:27:48-04:00** - The bottle raises the LIMITER by a tenth rather than the speed, which is the owner's own justification and the better mechanism: a gear's speed ceiling is its limiter expressed as a speed, so the extra speed is earned through the gearing the car already has rather than granted beside it. It reverses a note that was wrong on the physics - top speed is where power balances drag, not where drag acts alone - and wrong on the game, because in the final gear a bottle aimed at the car's own top end bought nothing in the one place a player saves it for. The measurement was wrong four times before it was right: a ceiling is measured from above, on an empty road, with the throttle held.

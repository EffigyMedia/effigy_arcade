### RLG-042 — Verify the physics is uniform across player, racers and traffic, and that the claimed figures are real
- **RULED 2026-08-28T17:33:06-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-28, two rulings.**

**1. "Verify that the car stats are uniform between the player and the racers. All cars, even the
traffic, play off the same reality and physics."**

**2. "Apart from the rubber banding, racer vehicles should mirror the stats of the player-capable
vehicles. Traffic cars all have real stats as well, except their drivers are the limitation."**

So the target state is one physics for everything on the road. What separates a rival from a taxi is
the CAR and the DRIVER, not two different sets of rules. The rubber band is the single, named
exception, and it is [[RLG-038]]'s, already measured.

**A code read - NOT yet a measurement - says the target state is a long way off. There are three
models, and a fourth for the garage card:**

| | acceleration | top speed | uses its own mass, hp, pull |
|---|---|---|---|
| player | `1000 * gearFactor() * pull` | `MAX_SPD * vmax` | `pull` yes; mass and hp for display only |
| racers | `2850 * aiGearFactor(v, top)` | an `AI_TOP` ceiling | **no** |
| traffic | none - a `cruise` set once at spawn | a random range by type STRING | **no** - never reads `BODY` |

Three specific findings, each to be proved before it is acted on:

- **`aiGearFactor` calls `gearTable()` and `redline()`, and both read `optBody` - the PLAYER's car.**
  A rival's acceleration is therefore computed through the player's gearbox and rev limit. A rival
  in a four-speed MUSCLE accelerates through a six-speed box if that is what the player chose.
- **A rival's `pull` is dead.** An exhaustive search for `.pull` returns three matches: inside
  `zeroSixty()`, the unrelated gear-ratio field `G.pull`, and `r.pull = B.pull` at road.js:5212,
  which is assigned to every rival and never read. This is a positive result rather than a failure
  to find something, so it is evidence.
- **Traffic never touches `BODY`.** A coupe in traffic cruises at `rnd(0.26, 0.42) * MAX_SPD`, keyed
  off the string `'coupe'`. `BODY.COUPE` exists only for when the player unlocks and drives one.

**And the claimed figures do not match the table.** The rung comment at road.js:1451 states MUSCLE
184mph and TUNER 172mph; the table gives `vmax` 0.94 and 0.82, which are 188 and 164. The supercar
comment says the fastest car "sits AT" 200 and that anything above 1.0 was clamped; STALLION is 1.03
and FORMULA is 1.09, `carTop` is `MAX_SPD * vmax` with no clamp, so they do 206 and 218. And
`zeroSixty()`, which prints the card, is a fourth model: it approximates the torque curve as a
straight ramp rather than calling `gearFactor()`, and its own comment claims the driving code adds
`2850 * gearFactor * pull` when the driving code adds 1000.

**The decisive experiment, because it settles the first finding by effect rather than by reading:**
measure the rivals' acceleration with the player in a four-speed MUSCLE (redline 10,000) and again
in a six-speed FORMULA (redline 15,000), same seed, same grid. If the rivals' acceleration moves,
they are provably driving the player's gearbox. Nothing is changed until that has run.
- **RULED 2026-08-28T19:39:48-04:00** — **MEASURED, nothing changed.** `tools/stat-test.py` is new. Three experiments, each able to refute
the claim it tests, and the two decisive ones repeated in independent sessions.

**The headline is narrower and more precise than the code read that preceded it, and in one place
the code read was WRONG in the reassuring direction.** There are not simply "three models". The
player's physics is honest; the garage card lies mildly; the rivals borrow the player's gearbox; the
traffic has no physics at all.

---

**1. THE TABLE IS TRUTHFUL ABOUT TOP SPEED. THE COMMENTS AROUND IT ARE NOT.**

Every one of the fourteen driveable bodies reaches exactly `vmax * 200`, measured in cleared air:
delta **+0 for all fourteen**. So `vmax` is not an aspiration, it is what the car does.

That settles a disagreement this fragment previously recorded the wrong way round. The earlier entry
noted that the rung comment at road.js:1451 claims MUSCLE 184 and TUNER 172 while the table gives
188 and 164, and left open which was right. **The table is right. The comments are wrong**, and so
are two others: the supercar comment claiming the fastest car "sits AT" 200 and that anything above
1.0 was clamped - STALLION measured 206 and FORMULA 218, and there is no clamp - and the
SUPERCRUISER note claiming its equipment "costs 4mph" against a MATADOR, where the gap is 10.

**No comment is corrected in this unit.** They are wrong about numbers that [[RLG-043]] is about to
change, and rewriting them now would mean writing them twice.

**2. THE GARAGE CARD IS OPTIMISTIC, AND MORE SO THE SLOWER THE CAR.**

| car | card | measured | error |
|---|---|---|---|
| FORMULA | 2.7s | 2.7s | -0.0 |
| MATADOR | 2.9s | 3.1s | +0.1 |
| MUSCLE | 4.1s | 4.2s | +0.1 |
| ROADSTER | 5.1s | 5.5s | +0.4 |
| CAB | 8.9s | 9.5s | +0.6 |
| VAN | 11.8s | 13.0s | +1.3 |

The error is always in the same direction and grows with the time taken, which is the signature of
`zeroSixty()` approximating the torque curve as a straight ramp - `1.55 - min(1, frac*(6/gt))*1.05` -
instead of calling the `gearFactor()` the driving code uses. Its own comment claims the driving code
adds `2850 * gearFactor() * pull`; the driving code adds **1000**.

**LORRY is not measured and the blank is a HARNESS LIMIT, not a finding.** Its card says 23.1s and
the measurement window is 20s. Recorded so the gap in the table is never read as a car that failed to
move.

**3. THE RIVALS ACCELERATE THROUGH THE PLAYER'S GEARBOX.** Proven by effect.

One rival - the same CREST in both arms - held two miles back in clean air where the rubber band is
saturated and identical, knocked to 40% of its target and timed back to 90%. **Only the player's car
changed.**

| player's car | recovery, session 1 | session 2 |
|---|---|---|
| MUSCLE, four-speed | 3.82s | 3.82s |
| FORMULA, six-speed | 4.75s | 4.75s |
| difference | **+24.4%** | **+24.3%** |

Four runs per arm agreeing to within 0.01s, and two independent sessions agreeing to 0.1 of a
percentage point. `aiGearFactor` calls `gearTable()`, which reads `optBody`.

**What the signal is NOT.** The redline cancels exactly inside `aiGearFactor`: `rpm` is built as
`IDLE + (r/G.to)*(redline-IDLE)` and `torqueAt` divides by `(redline-IDLE)`, so the fraction is
`r/G.to` and the rev limit cannot be the cause. **Gear COUNT is** - four ratios against six.

This is also the second, independent confirmation that a rival ignores its own stats, alongside the
dead `r.pull` at road.js:5212.

**4. THE TRAFFIC NEVER READS THE TABLE, AND THE PROOF IMPROVED ON REPETITION.**

Observed cruise speed against what `BODY` declares for that vehicle:

| type | session 1 | session 2 |
|---|---|---|
| coupe | 0.43 | 0.43 |
| muscle | 0.42 | **0.29** |
| sedan | 0.48 | **0.32** |
| taxi | 0.57 | 0.57 |
| truck | 0.66 | 0.61 |

**The ratios are not merely far from 1.00 - they MOVE between sessions for the same type.** A road
reading `BODY` even loosely would put a given type near the same fraction every run. These do not,
because `cruise` is drawn from a random range keyed off a type STRING at spawn and `BODY` is never
consulted. The instability is stronger evidence than a stable wrong number would have been.

---

**WHAT THIS MEANS FOR [[RLG-043]], which is the reason it was measured.**

- **Lowering a traffic body's `vmax` will change nothing on the road.** The reduction the owner asked
  for cannot reach traffic until traffic reads the table. RLG-043 is blocked on that, not merely
  sequenced after it.
- **Lowering a sports car's `vmax` WILL work exactly as intended** for the player, because the table
  is truthful. It will also change what a rival in that car asks for, since `r.base` comes from
  `r.vmax` - but not how hard it accelerates, which is the player's gearbox and a flat 2850.
- **The card will keep being optimistic** unless `zeroSixty()` is made to call `gearFactor()`. After a
  reduction the printed figures move, and they will still be wrong by the same growing margin.

**Not measured, and worth stating so nobody assumes it was:** braking, grip and cornering. This unit
measured straight-line acceleration and top speed only. `brake` and `grip` are stats the owner may
also mean by "lower the stats a bit", and nothing here says whether they are honest.
- **RULED 2026-08-28T19:51:23-04:00** — **Third session, attested as [[EVD-009]].** The measurement above was written from two sessions; the

- **gearbox:** MUSCLE 3.82s against FORMULA 4.75s, **+24.2%**, alongside +24.4% and +24.3%. Three

Three sessions is above the bar [[RLG-033]] set for this project after its own numbers failed to
reproduce between invocations. These are established.
- **RULED 2026-08-28T19:53:01-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-28, hardening the ruling now that it is measured:** "I want all cars to run off the
same physical stat table."

**The measurement above says exactly how far that is, and the three parts are very different sizes:**

- **the player is already there.** Every body reaches its declared top speed exactly. Nothing to do.
- **the rivals are half there.** `r.base` comes from their own `vmax`, so what a rival ASKS for is
  its own car - but its acceleration is `2850 * aiGearFactor`, where `aiGearFactor` reads the
  PLAYER's `gearTable()`, and its own `pull`, `mass` and `hp` are unread. `r.pull` is assigned at
  road.js:5212 and never used. This is a repair, not a rewrite: the rival needs its own gearbox and
  its own torque, both of which the table already declares.
- **the traffic is not there at all.** It has no acceleration model and never reads `BODY`. This one
  IS a rewrite, and it is why [[RLG-043]]'s traffic half is blocked.

**Do them in that order, and measure between each.** `stat-test.py` exists now and its gearbox
experiment is the acceptance test for the middle one: when a rival runs its own gearbox, changing the
player's car must move that number to ZERO. It reads +24.2 to +24.4% today, across three sessions,
which makes it an unusually clean before-and-after.

**The traffic rewrite must land with [[RLG-054]] and [[RLG-045]], not before them.** Giving traffic
real physics without a driver to limit it produces a road where a coupe does 160mph. The stats and
the personality that restrains them are one change.

**And it interacts with [[RLG-053]].** That fragment rules one lamp path for every vehicle; this one
rules one physics path for every vehicle. They are the same instinct applied to two subsystems, and
neither should be built with an `if` that names a vehicle class.
- **RULED 2026-08-28T20:22:02-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-28:** "the stats do need to include brake and grip."

**So the measurement in this fragment is INCOMPLETE by the owner's scope, and knowingly so.** It
covered straight-line acceleration and top speed. `brake` and `grip` are unmeasured, and
[[RLG-055]] - the full fleet rewrite - is blocked on them for the reason this fragment demonstrated:
the `vmax` column turned out to be TRUE while three comments around it were false, and nobody could
have predicted which way that would fall. A `brake` figure lowered without knowing whether it was
honest is a change that cannot be interpreted afterwards.

**A fourth experiment is wanted in `stat-test.py`:** braking distance from a fixed speed to a stop,
and cornering, per body, against what `brake` and `grip` declare. Two notes for whoever builds it:

- **braking is measurable cleanly** and should be, on the same cleared air the acceleration test
  uses - a stop measured with a van in the way is a measurement of the van.
- **grip is the harder one and must not be measured with the drive-test autopilot.** That driver
  saws at the wheel, and this project has already been told once by a harness that Raceway's tyres
  died in twenty seconds when the autopilot was the cause. Cornering has to be measured with a
  steady, repeatable input or the number will be about the driver.
- **RULED 2026-08-28T20:32:40-04:00** — **PART 2 SHIPPED in v0.9.8: every AI car now runs its own gearbox and its own torque.**

`aiGearFactor` took a `top` from its caller and read `gearTable()` and `redline()`, both of which
read `optBody` - the player's car. `aiAccel` used a flat `2850` and no `pull` at all, which is why
`r.pull` was assigned to every rival and never read. Both are fixed:

    function aiGearFactor(v, key)          // the car's OWN table, redline and vmax
    function aiAccel(v, want, dt, key)     // 1000 * aiGearFactor(v, key) * pullOf(key)

`1000 * ... * pull` is the player's own expression, from road.js:6513. The gear reference moved with
it: it was the caller's `top` - the band's ceiling for a rival, the flat `AI_TOP` for a cruiser - and
is now `MAX_SPD * vmax`, which is what the player's `gearRpm` has always used. The two disagreed
about what a gear IS; they agree now.

The police were on the same broken path and are fixed with them: a cruiser drives a `CRUISER` and an
interceptor a `SUPERCRUISER`. Gear tables are cached by gear count, because eleven rivals a frame
rebuilding an array of objects is a lot of garbage for a table with three shapes.

**THE ACCEPTANCE TEST PASSES.** The same experiment that found the fault:

| player's car | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| MUSCLE, four-speed | 3.82s | 13.57s |
| FORMULA, six-speed | 4.75s | 13.52s |
| **difference** | **+24.2 to +24.4%** | **-0.4%** |

The player's car no longer reaches the rivals at all. That was the whole claim.

---

**BUT THE ABSOLUTE NUMBER MOVED BY 3.5x, AND THAT IS A BALANCE CHANGE THE OWNER MUST RULE ON.**

Recovery from 40% to 90% of pace went from 3.82s to 13.5s. This is not a defect - it is the size of
the fault that was being hidden. Rivals were accelerating on `2850` with no `pull` while every other
car on the road used `1000 * gearFactor * pull`, so they were pulling roughly three and a half times
harder than anything the player could buy. **The old figure was the wrong one.** But the race has
been tuned around it for as long as it has existed.

**It weakened the rubber band, measured, and the band is the one thing the owner excluded from
shared physics.** `band-test`, same instrument, before and after:

| rival held at | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| two miles behind (tow) | +13.21% | **+11.44%** |
| two miles ahead (governor) | -7.80% | **-3.21%** |
| towed rival above 180mph | 93% of samples | **0%** |

The band is not broken - it still moves `want` exactly as [[RLG-038]] made it. What changed is that a
rival can no longer ACCELERATE to the ceiling the tow raises for it inside the window the
measurement watches: it ran 172.7mph against a ceiling of 205. Both arms became
acceleration-limited rather than ceiling-limited, which is also why the governor's measured effect
halved - with both arms held back by the same slow acceleration, there is less room between them.

**Three ways forward, and it is the owner's call, not this unit's:**

1. **accept it.** Rivals now accelerate like cars in the same game. The band acts more slowly and
   still acts.
2. **raise `pull` for the rival-capable bodies** in [[RLG-055]]'s fleet table, which is being written
   for the owner's signature anyway. This is the honest place for it: `pull` is a published stat, so
   a rival that pulls harder is a car that pulls harder, and the garage card says so.
3. **give the AI an explicit multiplier** - a named, committed tunable rather than a constant hidden
   in an expression. Least honest of the three, because it reintroduces "AI cars are special" under a
   better name.

**Recommended: 2.** It puts the decision in the table the owner is already going to sign off, and it
cannot drift back into a hidden special case.

**Not measured, and worth not over-claiming:** the rival's new acceleration was checked against the
player's EXPRESSION, not against the player's measured 76-to-171mph time. The claim here is that
they now compute acceleration identically, not that a stopwatch has confirmed they match over that
interval.

**Green:** drive-test 25/25 across both driving games, circuit-test holds at a standstill, smoke-test
6/6, `pack.sh --check` all six gates, `node --check` clean.

### RLG-123 — Interstate rolls to the line and Motorsport starts from the grid, and that is the marque's main split
- **RULED 2026-08-31T15:49:03-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-31: "Maybe the whole thing would just make more sense if it was actually a rolling start where the car is just pinned to 50 miles an hour and then the countdown happens and it relinquishes control to the player. During races, you have the whole field of opponents doing the same ahead of you." And immediately after: "This would also provide the main difference between interstate and motorsport."

SO IT IS A DISCIPLINE, NOT A SETTING. INTERSTATE IS THE ENDLESS ROAD: you are already driving when a run begins, the count happens while the car is pinned at fifty, and GO changes exactly one thing - who is steering. MOTORSPORT IS A CIRCUIT: a race starts from a grid, standing, on the neutral launch and the rev window of [[RLG-110]]. Each machine gets the start that belongs to it, and neither is a lesser version of the other.

IT IS ONE NUMBER ON THE `CFG` SEAM. `CFG.rollStartMph` - Interstate sets 50, Motorsport says nothing, and nothing means a grid. There is no branch anywhere that names a machine, which is what the seam is for: `road.js` still does not know which of its two games is running.

INTERSTATE HAD TO SET SOMETHING, and that is worth noting because its own file says it "passes almost nothing - which is the point. Every seam it leaves unset falls through to the behaviour it always had." The rolling start IS the behaviour it always had: `reset` opened with `gear = 2; spd = MAX_SPD * 0.155` and a note explaining that a dead stop in first meant four seconds of nothing while the car got out of its own way. RLG-088 put a count in front of the run and pinned the speed to zero through it, and those two lines sat there for weeks describing a start the game no longer had. They mean something again.

WHAT ROLLS AND WHAT DOES NOT. The car is pinned at the start speed rather than at zero, and the road passes under it - measured at 10,318 units over a count, which is fifty miles an hour and not a readout over a frozen world. The ODOMETER does not move, and that separation is the point: distance unlocks cars, and the count is not the player's to spend. The run clock does not run either.

THE FIELD DOES WHAT YOU DO. In a race every rival is pinned to the same fifty and holds station, so its position relative to the player does not change by a yard while the count is up. That is `rollField`, and it is deliberately NOT `stepRacers` with the throttle shut: a rival being STEPPED is choosing lanes, reading the cars in front of it and building toward its own target speed, and none of that is a car waiting for a countdown. Measured: every rival within 0 units of the roll, no rival changing speed by more than 0 over a second of the count, and the largest speed step at GO itself 117 against 944 gained over the following second - so nothing snaps at the line.

THE AUTOMATIC GEARBOX SPLITS THE SAME WAY. On a grid it is held out of gear, because the launch needs a neutral to be dropped from. On a rolling start `autoGear` runs, because a car doing fifty has to be in the gear that suits fifty.

AND THE LAUNCH FURNITURE ONLY APPEARS WHERE THERE IS A LAUNCH. The amber band on the tachometer and the NEUTRAL / REV IT strip are gated on the launch being ARMED rather than on the count being up, so Interstate shows neither. A strip telling a rolling player to rev would be instructions for a different game.

PROVED, AND THE PROOF IS NOT VACUOUS. `tools/launch-test.py` now tests each start where it lives - the window on Motorsport, the roll on Interstate - and `tools/start-test.py`'s held check was rewritten rather than deleted. IT USED TO SAY "the car does not move", which was right while every start was standing and would now be the fault rather than the proof. What that check was ever ABOUT survives unchanged: a countdown drawn over a car that is already ACCELERATING is a lie the first frame gives away. So it asserts the speed does not move, with the pedal down throughout - measured 3833 to 3833 units across the whole count.

Setting `rollStartMph` back to 0 by hand fails the three checks that depend on the roll. The two that still pass at zero are about PINNING rather than about rolling, and the guard against that vacuous case is the explicit check that the machine has a rolling speed at all.

A CAP STATED RATHER THAN HIDDEN. [[RLG-118]]'s standing grid - eleven cars at rest on a circuit - is no longer covered by any harness and cannot be: Motorsport's DRIVE is a solo practice session and QUALIFY is a solo lap, so neither reaches a grid. Reaching a circuit grid from a harness is unsolved and drive-test does not do it either. The harness prints that it is not covered rather than passing quietly.

NOT VERIFIED FROM HERE: whether fifty is the right speed to be handed the car at, and whether a rolling start reads as a start at all rather than as the run simply having begun. Both are the owner's on the device, and fifty is one config row.

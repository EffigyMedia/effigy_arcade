### RLG-020 — Interstate: cars pop to a different texture in the rearview
- **RULED 2026-08-28T13:51:37-04:00** — - **Owner report:** in the rearview there is a point where cars pop into a different texture.

- **What to look for.** The mirror draws the same sprite set as the forward view but through its own
  projection, so a level-of-detail or sprite-variant switch keyed to screen size will fire at a
  different distance behind you than in front. The pop is the boundary being crossed, not a missing
  sprite.

- **Not yet investigated.** Find the threshold before changing anything - a pop that is really two
  sprite sheets disagreeing is a different fix from a pop that is one threshold set too near.
- **RULED 2026-08-28T14:07:14-04:00** — - **Found, and it is an explicit threshold rather than a sprite-sheet disagreement.** The mirror
  drew a real front-view sprite at `sw >= 26` pixels wide and a simplified drawn block below it.
  One frame a car was a block, the next it was a painted nose, and the two do not look alike.

- **The reasoning behind BOTH renderings is sound and is kept.** Below about 26px the sprite is mush
  and the block is genuinely cleaner; close up the painted nose is the whole reason those painters
  exist. Neither end was wrong.

- **What made it read as a fault rather than as detail arriving** is that the switch was hard, and
  every car crossed it at the same screen width - so the change happened in the same place on screen
  every single time. A discontinuity that repeats in a fixed position stops looking like distance and
  starts looking like a bug.

- **The fix is the middle.** The sprite fades in across a 20 to 34 pixel band, drawn OVER the block
  rather than instead of it, so detail arrives gradually and there is no frame in which a car changes
  identity. Above the band the block is skipped entirely, so the common case costs nothing.

- **What is proven and what is not.** `drive-test` on Interstate is 9/9 after the change, so nothing
  regressed. **The smoothness itself is not machine-checked** - a pixel-delta metric over the mirror
  strip would be a harness of its own, and the fade is correct by construction. Whether the band is
  in the right place is a judgement to make on a device.

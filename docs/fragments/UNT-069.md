### UNT-069 - The shoreline is one line from the horizon to the bumper

- Routing class: `defect` -> **claude-opus-5**, effort **high**. Bias 1. Finding the cause is the work, and a wrong guess costs a second pass. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-08-30T16:22:21-04:00** - The audit's AUD-002 was mostly a defect in the check. A picture of the frame it failed on shows a clean straight diagonal: the metric took the largest step between neighbouring rows, which only means anything while the edge is steeper than 45 degrees, so a shallow shoreline reported a 121px staircase on a correct picture. Three more faults followed - it compared rows that do not touch, a second difference is large on any bend, and a local line fit cannot tell a staircase from a crest, where the brow legitimately hides the slices between two shorelines. The test is now outliers against the edge's own median step: 0 in five runs with the quad, 6 to 8 with the rectangle, and it catches the rectangle three times in four, which is a miss rather than a false alarm. One real fault was found on the way and it was not the one named: a slice a third of a pixel tall was projecting its tail across the frame, because a slope divided by a sub-pixel height is a rounding error made into a line.

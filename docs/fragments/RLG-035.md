### RLG-035 — Redline: horns and sirens do not work
- **RULED 2026-08-28T16:06:06-04:00** — - **Owner report:** horns and sirens do not seem to work - specifically, the effect they should have
  on traffic never happens.

- **THE SOUND WAS NEVER THE PROBLEM, and that was measured first.** Driving the button and reading the
  gain nodes: horn 0.085 while held, 0 released; siren 0.06 with the light bar on; every voice
  present, no errors. The audio path was correct all along, so the fault was entirely in the effect.

- **The design the owner remembered is all there, and it is right.** Each car carries `heed`, starting
  at 1, multiplied by 0.62 every time it is asked and refuses and by 0.86 when it obeys, recovering
  at 0.14 a second once you are past. The odds are 0.40 for a player's horn and 0.90 for a siren -
  both the light bar and a cruiser's. **Nothing about that needed changing.** It was invisible
  because of three separate faults in the code that fed it.

- **1. It compared a lane INDEX to a road position.** `Math.abs(c.lane - ol) > 0.6`, where `ol` is a
  lateral position in the range -1 to 1 and `c.lane` is 0 to 3. A car in lane 3 read as 3 away from a
  player in the middle of the road and was never asked; a car in lane 0 read as 0 away and was asked
  wherever it actually was. **The comment above that line records an earlier fix to the same line** -
  `ol` had been corrected from a lane to a position, and `c.lane` was left as it was. Half-fixed.

- **2. A car that agreed to move did not move.** It reassigned `c.lane` and stopped. But `lane` is a
  label; what is drawn, collided with and seen is `c.x`, and nothing moved it. Both halves of the
  function were wrong in the same direction: one compared an index to a position, the other wrote an
  index and expected a position to follow.

- **3. The search started at the camera.** `oz` defaulted to `pos`, and the player sits `PLAYER_Z`
  further down the road - about 880 units - so a window of 40 to 1500 ahead of `pos` was really 840
  BEHIND the car to 620 in front of it. **This one was horn-only**, because a siren passes the
  cruiser's own `fromZ`. The other two broke both.

- **A car asked to move accepts a tighter gap than one changing lanes for convenience**, which is what
  people do - so `laneClear` takes an urgency that shrinks the margin, and never to zero.

- **MEASURED RATHER THAN GUESSED, after two wrong guesses.** Instrumenting the refusal reasons over 40
  horn presses: 6 cars actually in range, 3 refused on the dice, 1 had nowhere to go, 2 moved. The
  bottleneck is simply how often you are tucked behind somebody - the system itself is working as
  designed.

- **AN OPEN ISSUE, RECORDED RATHER THAN PAPERED OVER.** Scatter and the keep-a-lane-open guarantee
  pull against each other, and the corridor measurement varies between runs: **0.227, 0.323, 0.363,
  0.418, 0.428, 0.452** against a 0.34 limit. Two of those are under it. A swept-path check was tried
  and made it worse (0.323 to 0.227) and was reverted. **The guarantee is therefore not absolute
  while the horn is in use**, and that needs its own unit rather than another guess at the end of
  this one.

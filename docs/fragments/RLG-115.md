### RLG-115 — A production or utility vehicle can only go on TEST DRIVE
- **RULED 2026-08-31T11:44:39-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-31, recorded for a later queue and not started: "if you have a production or utility
vehicle selected, the only mode available is test drive."

IT IS A RULE ABOUT WHAT A CAR IS FOR, AND IT MAKES THE CLASSES MEAN SOMETHING. The garage already
divides the fleet - super, formula, cruiser, production, utility - and today those names decide only
when a car unlocks. This gives them a second job: the ordinary cars are for driving around in, and the
events are for the cars built to enter them. A saloon lining up on a starting grid against three
supercars is a race nobody wrote.

AND IT MAKES THE AMBULANCE AND THE BUS COHERENT, which is the thing to notice given [[RLG-114]] was
agreed an hour before this. A driveable bus that could enter a tournament would be a joke asset; a
driveable bus that can only go out on the open road is a thing worth having. This ruling is what makes
that whole class of vehicle sensible rather than novelty, so the two belong in the same queue.

WHERE IT LIVES AND WHAT IT MUST NOT BECOME. The mode buttons are on the title, the car is chosen in
the garage, and the two are separate screens - so the gate has to survive the player choosing a car
AFTER choosing a mode, as well as before. The wrong build is a check inside each mode's start; the
right one is the menu not offering what the car cannot do, so the rule is visible rather than
enforced by refusal. This project's own standard is that a gesture is taught in play the first time it
matters, and a greyed-out button with a reason on it teaches better than a button that does nothing.

THE OPEN QUESTION IS WHAT THE PLAYER SEES, and it should be asked rather than guessed. Three shapes,
and they are not equivalent: the other modes disappear; they grey out with a line saying why; or the
garage refuses the car while a race mode is selected. The second is likeliest to be right because it
explains itself, but it is the owner's call.

AND ONE THING TO CHECK BEFORE BUILDING: what happens to a SAVED selection. If a player leaves the
game with a bus selected and a tournament in progress, the next launch has to resolve that
disagreement somehow. The cleanest answer is that the mode is the thing that persists and the car
follows it, but that is a decision rather than an obvious consequence.

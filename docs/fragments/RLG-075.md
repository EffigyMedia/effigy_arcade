### RLG-075 — The instruments capped the formula cars below their own top speed
- **RULED 2026-08-29T10:31:39-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-29: the tachometer and/or the speedometer artificially cap the formula cars from
reaching their true potential.

The tachometer does not - it scales to each car's own redline and its own top speed, and was checked.
Two other things did, and both were constants written when the quickest car in the game did 206mph:

1. A HARD CLAMP ON SPEED at `MAX_SPD * 1.30`, which is 260mph. It is a safety clamp - the only thing
   it should ever stop is a number going wild - and it was stopping a CAR. COMET's declared top end
   is 276mph, so the fastest car in the game could not reach its own figure, and nothing in the code
   said why. APEX at 260 sat exactly on it, so a slipstream bought it nothing at all.

2. THE SPEEDOMETER FACE, fixed at 260mph. That number was itself a fix for an earlier version fixed
   at 200, which is the same mistake with a smaller number: the needle pegs, and a pegged needle is
   indistinguishable from a car that has stopped accelerating.

Both are derived from the fleet now - the highest `vmax` in `BODY`, plus a tenth for the slipstream
and a frame of overshoot on the clamp, and rounded up to the next twenty on the dial. A body added
later moves them with it, which is the only way a number like this stays true.

THE RED BAND IS THE CAR'S OWN CEILING NOW rather than a fixed 200mph. Above its `vmax` a car is
over-running: the engine is past what its gearing and its aero can hold and it sheds speed hard the
moment you lift. That is a fact about the car you are in, so the dial says it about the car you are
in.

MEASURED, BOTH WAYS. With the fix: VECTOR 248 of 248, APEX 260 of 260, COMET 276 of 276, STALLION 206
of 206. With the old clamp put back: COMET reaches 260 of 276. The check is not vacuous.

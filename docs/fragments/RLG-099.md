### RLG-099 — The tyres screech when the car slides in a turn
- **RULED 2026-08-31T08:35:31-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-31: "I also feel like the tire should screech if the car is slid across the ground
because of the centripetal forces of a turn."

IT IS THE SAME SOUND WITH A SECOND CAUSE, WHICH IS WHY IT SITS BESIDE RLG-098 AND NOT INSIDE IT. A
tyre squeals when rubber slides over tarmac. Braking makes it slide along the car's length; cornering
makes it slide across. The engine already knows how hard the car is cornering and already knows how
much grip it has, so the quantity exists - what is missing is that only one of the two ways of
exceeding it makes a noise.

DO THEM IN ORDER, AND THE ORDER IS NOT NEGOTIABLE. RLG-098 fixes what drives the squeal's PITCH.
Adding a second trigger first would mean tuning a cornering squeal against a pitch that is about to
change, and the tuning would have to be thrown away.

WHAT IT SHOULD BE DRIVEN BY. The lateral slip - how far the car is travelling sideways relative to
where it is pointing - against the grip available, which `wetGrip()` already reduces in rain and
snow. That gives the behaviour a driver expects for nothing: the same corner squeals earlier on a wet
road, and a car with more grip stays quiet longer. A branch that names a vehicle class to decide
whether it squeals would be exactly the thing three separate owner rulings have already forbidden -
the car is capable, the driver decides.

AND IT MUST NOT SQUEAL AT EVERY CORNER. The sound means "you are at the limit". If it plays whenever
the wheel is turned it stops carrying information and becomes noise, which is the same failure the
indicator work was warned about. There has to be a threshold, and it should be the grip limit rather
than a steering angle.

THE HARNESS CANNOT STEER TO THE LIMIT WITHOUT LYING ABOUT IT. `drive-test`'s autopilot saws at the
wheel - this project has already been told once that Raceway's tyres died in twenty seconds and the
autopilot was the cause. A check for this needs a steady, deliberate cornering input, not the
autopilot, and it should read the slip the engine computes rather than infer it from the sound.

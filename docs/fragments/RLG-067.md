### RLG-067 — The road cars are earned by distance on the clock, one class at a time
- **RULED 2026-08-29T08:41:10-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-29. Recorded as BUILT in v0.9.28 - the road cars are earned by distance now.**

> "I want to remove the original trigger for unlocking traffic cars, and make a new one: driving 25+
> miles in test drive mode with the timed turned on unlocks the utility. Driving 50+ miles in test
> drive mode with the timed turned on unlocks the production vehicles."

**What it replaces:** a hundred miles on TEST DRIVE with any settings at all, which opened every road
car at once. One enormous wall, and then nothing.

**What it is now**, and both need the clock RUNNING:

| distance | class |
|---|---|
| 25 miles | UTILITY - pickup, van, lorry |
| 50 miles | PRODUCTION - saloon, coupe, cab |

**The timer is the point of it.** Without it a hundred miles is something you can leave the game
doing; with it, distance has to be earned at checkpoints. And two thresholds mean the first arrives
early enough to be a promise rather than a rumour.

**Utility first is deliberate:** they are the odd ones to drive, and an ordinary saloon is the better
prize for going twice as far.

**A save that already holds the retired `traffic` flag keeps both classes.** Taking a car back off
somebody who earned it under the old rule would be the worst kind of change, so `openBy` honours the
old flag for either new lock.

**This uses the class taxonomy from [[RLG-053]]** - super, sport, police, production, utility - which
was built for the fleet sheet the same day. That is the second use of it, and it is why the classes
are on `API.fleet` rather than in the sheet: **a class is what a car IS, not how it is drawn.**

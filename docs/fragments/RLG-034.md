### RLG-034 — Redline: slipstream lets revs go past the limiter
- **RULED 2026-08-28T15:44:54-04:00** — - **Owner report:** slipstream works well, but it lets the revs go past the limiter, which should not
  happen. And, raised while the fix was being built: **the engine PITCH must not rise at the limiter
  either, because the pitch IS the rpm.**

- **The owner's model, which the fix follows exactly.** A slipstream may let you exceed your normal
  top speed - that is aerodynamics. It must not touch the engine or the transmission. So a tow raises
  the ceiling drag sets, and moves nothing about gearing, the limiter, or the note.

- **THREE SEPARATE CAUSES, and only the first was the one reported.**
  1. `Math.min(carTop * slip, gearCap * slip)` applied the tow to the GEAR ceiling as well as the
     aero one. `gearCap` is the limiter expressed as a speed, so scaling it moves the limiter. Now
     `Math.min(carTop * slip, gearCap)` - the aero ceiling lifts, the gearing does not.
  2. **`gearRpm` clamped to `redline() + 300`.** The dial was permitted to sit 300rpm past the
     limiter whenever speed ran over the gear's ceiling, with or without a tow - and this, not the
     slipstream, is what puts the needle past the mark. A rev limiter is not a suggestion; the
     neutral path a few lines away already models one properly, cutting in and out BELOW the line.
  3. **The engine note was computed against a slip-reduced divisor.** The call passed
     `MAX_SPD * (1 - slipT*0.22)` as `top`, and pitch is `spd / top`, so a tow lowered the divisor
     and the note climbed while the revs sat pinned. The intent behind that expression is recorded at
     the call site and it was about the WIND - dirty air is quieter and rougher. That is true and is
     kept: `slip` is its own argument now and touches the wind alone.

- **THE GUARD FAILED TO DISCRIMINATE THREE TIMES, and that is the lesson.** With the bug present it
  reported 97.4%, then 98.6%, then 96.7% - passing every time, for the same reason each time: **the
  autopilot never happened to reach the limiter inside the sample window**, so the ceiling was never
  tested. Forcing a tow was not enough either.

  A guard that only fires when the driver happens to arrive somewhere is not a guard. The invariant
  is about the ceiling, so the car is now PUT on the ceiling - full tow, speed pushed past anything
  the gearing allows - and whatever the dial reads there is the clamp. It discriminates now:
  **102.5% with the slack restored, 100.0% without.**

- **Proof.** `drive-test` 23/23 across both games, including the new limiter check. Verified failing
  against the reintroduced defect.

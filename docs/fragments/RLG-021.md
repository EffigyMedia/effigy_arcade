### RLG-021 — Interstate: a vehicle partly hidden by the curving road vanishes entirely
- **RULED 2026-08-28T13:51:38-04:00** — - **Owner report:** the intent was to obscure vehicles PARTIALLY when the curving road hides part of
  them. They currently disappear completely the moment any part is obscured.

- **Why this is worth doing properly.** A car that vanishes because its wheels went behind a crest
  reads as a rendering fault; a car whose bottom half is cut off reads as a hill. The second one is
  also information - it tells you how far over the crest the car is.

- **The likely shape of the fix.** Clip the sprite to the visible band rather than skipping the draw.
  The occlusion test is presumably already computing where the road edge is; what it does with that
  is a `continue` where it should be a clip rectangle.
- **RULED 2026-08-28T14:16:12-04:00** — - **The partial-clip code already existed and could never run.** `crestY()` was deliberately
  returning `null`, so both the cull and the clip below it were unreachable, and the only occlusion
  in force was the bucket order - which is slice-granular, so a car was either fully emitted or not
  emitted at all. That is the all-or-nothing the owner saw.

- **The note left behind said why it was switched off, and that reason has expired.** It said a
  single scalar per segment cannot express visibility, and that the real answer was to interleave
  sprites with the road slices. **That interleave has since been built** - sprites are emitted during
  the road pass now. What was left switched off was the part that was never right.

- **THE SIGN WAS THE WHOLE BUG.** In this projection further away is HIGHER on screen - a smaller y.
  A crest between you and a car covers it FROM THE BOTTOM UP, so what survives is the roof, and the
  visible band is ABOVE the crest line. The old code clipped to `rect(0, brow, W, H - brow)`, the band
  BELOW it. Exactly inverted, which is why it hid the wrong half, and why flipping the comparison
  three times never fixed it.

- **Three cases now, and only one costs a clip.** Entirely under the silhouette is culled; entirely
  above it draws as before; across it is drawn clipped to the band above the crest. **A car cut off
  at the waist reads as a hill**, and it is information - how much of it you can see says how far over
  the brow it is.

- **The engine had no observability, which is why this was wrong twice invisibly.** Both previous
  attempts left the road driving and the console clean, and one of them culled every distant sprite -
  the source note records "23 cars in range, 0 drawn". So `spriteStats` now counts drawn, clipped and
  culled per frame; `ROAD()` publishes its API as `window.__road` rather than returning it into
  nothing; and `tools/occlusion-test.py` drives and reads them.

- **PROVEN BOTH WAYS.** With the fix: drawn 347, clipped 31, culled 11 - cars are being cut off
  rather than deleted, and the road is not empty. With `crestY` disabled again: drawn 366,
  **clipped 0**, and the check fails. The assertion measures the fix rather than passing regardless.

- **One trap cost a run.** The first version of the test clicked through the title and got nowhere:
  `sw.js` claims the client on activate and `arcade.js` reloads on `controllerchange`, so anything
  clicked in the first second lands on a document about to be thrown away. The drive harness already
  waits that out; this one does now too.

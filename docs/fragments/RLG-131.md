### RLG-131 — A collision answers WHERE it happened, and moves both cars
- **RULED 2026-08-31T21:03:31-04:00** — OWNER, 2026-08-31, after playing through all the tournaments: "When you get into a collision, what
happens to your car should be based off of where the collision happened. Right now, no matter where
you get hit your car loses a ton of momentum. If you run into someone's rear end, that makes sense -
but should also send the car you hit flying forward. If you sideswipe somebody, it should just bump
you in the opposite direction of the impact as well as the car that you impact it. If someone hits
you from behind, they should get slowed down and you should get pushed forward. Right now if I cut
off an opponent and they hit me, I lose all my speed and they drive by me which kind of defeats the
entire purpose of defensive maneuvering."

THE DIAGNOSIS IS ONE SENTENCE: THE GEOMETRY IS ALREADY COMPUTED AND THEN THROWN AWAY. Both collision
sites work out where the other car is and immediately discard the sign.

TRAFFIC. `const dz = c.z - pz` is computed, and the only use of it is
`Math.abs(dz) < (c.len+380)/2` - the overlap test. The response that follows reads no geometry at
all:

    spd = Math.min(spd*0.55, c.spd*0.80);      // drop behind them so we separate

That single line is the whole of the owner's complaint. It is UNCONDITIONAL, so being rear-ended
gives the identical result to rear-ending. It takes the MINIMUM of two reductions, so a slow car
ahead drags the player down to 80% of ITS speed however the contact happened. And `c.spd` is never
written, so the other car is untouched: it does not get shoved forward when hit from behind and it
does not slow when it runs into the player. Laterally, `playerX` is pushed and `c.x` is not - so even
a sideswipe moves only one of the two cars.

RIVALS are better and still wrong in the same way. Both cars are pushed apart laterally, both lose
speed, and the shunt scales with the closing speed - but there is still no `dz` sign, so the response
is identical whoever ran into whom. And the split is against the player: `spd *= 0.80 - closing*0.10`
against `r.spd *= 0.86`. A RIVAL WHO REAR-ENDS THE PLAYER TAKES LESS FROM THE CONTACT THAN THE PLAYER
DOES, which is precisely the "they drive by me" the owner describes, stated as two constants.

THE THREE CASES THE OWNER NAMES, AND WHAT EACH NEEDS:

    you rear-end them      you lose speed - which is what happens today and is right - AND they are
                           shoved forward. `c.spd` / `r.spd` gains from the impact instead of being
                           left alone.
    you sideswipe them     neither car should lose much forward speed at all. Both get a lateral
                           bump away from the contact. Today the player loses up to 45% of their
                           speed for a rub.
    they rear-end you      THEY lose speed and YOU are pushed forward. Today this is the same branch
                           as the first case and it costs the player everything.

WHICH CASE IT IS, is a question the file already knows how to ask. The cop code computes
`sideOn = Math.abs(pdz) < (k.len + 380)/2 * 0.55` to tell an alongside contact from a nose-to-tail
one, for the PIT manoeuvre. The same shape works here: the SIGN of `dz` says who is in front, and the
MAGNITUDE relative to the combined length says whether it was a square hit or a rub.

MOMENTUM IS THE FRAME TO BUILD IT IN, rather than three branches with their own numbers. A rear-end
transfers speed from the faster car to the slower one along the road; a sideswipe transfers almost
nothing along the road and a lot across it. One expression in the closing speed and the overlap
fraction gives all three of the owner's cases, and it will not need re-tuning when a fourth case
appears.

AND THE STANDING RULING APPLIES. "The car is CAPABLE, the driver DECIDES" - so this must not become a
branch naming traffic or rivals. Mass is the honest input if the cases need to differ, because a
lorry shrugging off a hatchback is physics rather than a class rule. Note that traffic and rivals are
two separate code paths today and this change is the argument for making them one.

WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND REALISM, in the owner's words: defensive driving currently punishes the
defender. Cutting a rival off is the correct racing move and the engine charges the player for it.

NOT STARTED. Nothing about the collision response has been changed.
- **RULED 2026-08-31T22:28:41-04:00** — BUILT, 0.10.43. All three of the owner's cases work, and they are ONE EXPRESSION rather than three
branches.

THE RELATIVE SPEED IS WHO RAN INTO WHOM, and that is the finding. Catching a slower car makes it
positive; being caught by a faster one makes it negative; and the same subtraction then takes speed
off one car and puts it on the other in the right direction both times:

    const rel   = spd - o.spd;
    const along = rel * square * IMPACT.along;
    spd   -= along * share;
    o.spd += along * (1 - share);

No branch names a case, which matters because a fourth case would otherwise need a fourth branch -
and because three standing rulings in this project forbid exactly that shape.

`square` IS WHERE THE HIT LANDED: 1 nose-to-tail, 0 fully alongside, read from how far apart the two
cars are along the road at the moment of contact. A square hit exchanges speed along the road and
barely pushes sideways; a rub does the opposite. One number separates a rear-ending from a sideswipe.

MASS COMES OFF THE VEHICLE'S OWN DIMENSIONS, not from a class table. Every vehicle already carries a
width and a length, so a lorry shrugs off a hatchback because it is a bigger object rather than
because the code knows what a lorry is. Anything declaring a real mass keeps it.

MEASURED, staged rather than driven into, at 80% and 55% of top speed:

    you rear-end them     you -2736, them +2303
    they rear-end you     you +2736, them -2303
    you sideswipe them    you 0, them 0; shifted apart by 0.201 and 0.169
    into a lorry          you -2837, it gains only +1418

THE SECOND ROW IS THE OWNER'S ACTUAL REPORT and the first two being exact opposites is the ruling in
one line. Before this they were the same line of code, so cutting a rival off cost the defender
everything and the rival drove past.

ALL THREE COLLISION SITES NOW SHARE IT - traffic, rivals and cruisers. Leaving the cruiser on its own
path would have recreated the inconsistency this ruling exists to remove. The PIT manoeuvre is
untouched: it returns before reaching the collision.

RIVALS KEEP THEIR SMALLER DAMAGE and lose their unfair split. What was there pushed both cars apart
and took speed off both - closer to right than the traffic path - but it still read no geometry, and
the split was AGAINST the player at 0.80 against 0.86. A rival who rear-ended you lost less than you
did, which is "they drive by me" stated as two constants.

DAMAGE FOLLOWS THE HIT NOW instead of being a constant. A gentle rub is about a quarter of a square
hit at a big closing speed.

AND A SHUNT CLEARS THE SLIP OFFSET (see [[RLG-048]]), because a shunt is not something you steered.
That is a small rule with a real consequence and it was found by a check going red: slip-test's
three-second hold was being interrupted by traffic spawning behind the parked car, and the collision
correctly wiped the offset the test was measuring. The harness now keeps the road empty for the whole
reading.

`tools/impact-test.py` is new. It stages a collision at a chosen geometry rather than driving into
one and hoping - the subject is WHERE the hit landed, so driving into one measures the traffic AI
instead. It also asserts that nothing is CONJURED: what one car gains the other loses, within the
mass weighting, so a collision cannot become a speed source a player will find. One of its own
assertions was wrong first time and is recorded in the file: it asserted the SIGNS of the lateral
push, which is a claim about which side the other car was staged on rather than about the physics.

drive-test IS NOT SETTLED AND I WILL NOT CLAIM IT IS. Its nitrous assertion - "the bottle can be held
open for the check" - failed on two of three runs of this build and on one of three runs of the
commit before it. Both arms fail it and the samples are far too small to separate them, so this
change is NOT shown to be innocent and is NOT shown to be guilty. What can be said: the check reports
that the nitrous button did not take, which is a state it is written to notice rather than a
consequence this change has any evident path to. It needs more runs than a sprint can afford, and it
is recorded rather than dismissed.

smoke-test 6 of 6, slip-test all pass, impact-test all pass.

NOT JUDGED ON A DEVICE. Whether being shoved forward by a rival reads as satisfying rather than as
the game pushing you, whether the lorry ratio is enough, and whether a rub now costs too little are
all feel. `API.impactModel` is live so they can be dialled without a rebuild.

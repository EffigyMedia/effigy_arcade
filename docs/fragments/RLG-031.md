### RLG-031 — Interstate: cars still pop in and out whole, and some spawn in view
- **RULED 2026-08-28T15:17:20-04:00** — - **Owner report, after RLG-021 shipped:** the partial clip is mostly working, but there is still
  some total pop in and out. And some cars appear to spawn while already in view - they should come
  in beyond the focal point of the road, before they could be visible.

- **These are probably two different faults, and the second is the likelier cause of the first.**
  A car that spawns inside the draw distance appears from nothing, which looks exactly like a car
  popping in. Fix the spawn distance first and re-watch before touching the clip again.

- **What to check on the spawn side.** `spawnWave`, `spawnBehind`, `spawnTrap`, `spawnSuper` and
  `spawnCop` each choose their own z. `DRAW` is 150 segments of `SEG` 200, so the road is drawn to
  30,000 units - anything spawned nearer than that is spawned on screen. `drawSprite` also refuses
  anything closer than 430 units, which is the near clip, not the far one.

- **What to check on the clip side.** `crestY` returns the crest silhouette per segment index, and a
  car crossing a segment boundary can move between a clipped and an unclipped state in one frame.
  The band is per SEGMENT, so the silhouette can step rather than slide - which would read as a pop
  even though the clip itself is working.

- **Measure before changing.** `spriteStats` already counts drawn, clipped and culled per frame, so a
  sudden change in the culled count between consecutive frames is the pop, and it can be caught
  rather than watched for.

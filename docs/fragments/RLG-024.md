### RLG-024 — Interstate: widen the road so the lanes are bigger
- **RULED 2026-08-28T13:51:40-04:00** — - **Owner report:** the road needs to be a bit wider to make the lanes bigger.

- **What it touches.** `ROAD` is the half-width and `LANE_X` places four lanes inside it. Widening
  changes the projection of every sprite, the overlap tests for collisions, the traffic spawn
  geometry, the roadblock gap, and the verge/barrier limits at 1.15. **Do not widen `ROAD` alone** -
  check each of those, because several are written in lane units and several in world units.

### RLG-013 — Quietus was drawn as IET: the rename outran its own alphabet
- **RULED 2026-08-28T12:47:07-04:00** — - **The defect.** Quietus drew its title on the wall as **`IET`**. The word is set in the cabinet's
  own hand-painted blood alphabet, and that alphabet was authored for **DERELICT** - it held
  C D E I L R T and nothing else. `QUIETUS` needs Q, U and S. `Arcade.wordmark` renders an unknown
  character as `G[ch] || []`, which draws nothing and says nothing, so four of the seven letters were
  simply absent.

- **It was introduced by the rename in [[RLG-006]] and shipped through four releases.** The rename
  moved every string correctly; what it could not know is that one of those strings is drawn from a
  seven-letter alphabet. **This is the cost of a custom typeface that lives in one file and covers
  exactly one word.**

- **Every gate passed the whole time, and each for a defensible reason.** `pack.sh` checks that files
  parse and that the catalogue matches - a glyph map is data, and the data was valid. `smoke-test`
  asserts the canvas has real paint on it, and **a canvas with three letters on it has paint on it
  exactly like a canvas with seven.** No check was wrong. The thing they measured was not the thing
  that broke.

- **The fix is in two halves, and the second half matters more.**
  1. Q, U and S are drawn, in the same hand: a bowl with the tail struck through it, an open U with
     the weight in the bottom turn, and an S built from two reversed curves with the join where the
     brush lifts. D, R, L and C stay although nothing spells with them now - they are hand-authored
     letterforms, this is the only copy, and deleting art to tidy a map is not a trade worth making.
  2. **`Arcade.wordmark` now reports a missing glyph on the console**, once per word, naming the
     characters. It only does this when a cabinet supplies its own `glyphs`, because the shell's own
     alphabet is complete.

- **And the harness was taught to care.** `smoke-test.py` collects **console** errors as well as page
  errors now. A cabinet that quietly fails to draw part of its own name is exactly the class of bug
  the harness was built to catch and was not catching.

- **PROVEN, not asserted.** With `U` removed from the alphabet again, the harness fails with the
  reason spelled out:

        FAIL  quietus  - no page errors
                         (console: Arcade.wordmark: "QUIETUS" needs U and this
                          alphabet has no glyph for it - they will not draw.)

  Restored, all four cabinets are clean with console errors counted, so nothing else was tripping it.

- **Proof.** `pack.sh` all gates, 40 files; `smoke-test` 6/6; the title was rendered and read.

### RLG-125 — One crate that gives everything the car can use, and no seconds where seconds are worthless
- **RULED 2026-08-31T18:16:03-04:00** — THREE CRATES WERE PROPOSED AND THE OWNER DROPPED THEM THEMSELVES. Recorded in full, because the idea is a good one and will be proposed again.

The proposal, owner 2026-08-31: "I wonder if we should have three different pick ups: repair, NOS, time. A white crate with a picture of what the reward is - a wrench, a clock face, an NOS bottle. If the vehicle you're driving can't be rewarded NOS then that crate never spawns."

WHAT WAS RIGHT ABOUT IT. It turns an automatic payout into a decision, which is the same reasoning that made the launch a rev WINDOW rather than a plain curve ([[RLG-110]]) - the correct play should not be obvious. It makes the reward legible BEFORE you take it rather than after, which is what makes it a choice at all. And it answers [[RLG-107]] structurally: the fleet's capability decides what the world contains, instead of the payout being filtered at the moment you touch it.

FOUR THINGS WERE PUT AGAINST IT, and the first is the one that decided it.

AT ONE CRATE EVERY 20 TO 34 SECONDS YOU NEVER CHOOSE. `nextCrateT = rnd(20, 34)`, one box, alternating shoulders. You almost never see two at once, so you take whatever appears - and the decision the design is FOR does not arise. Getting it would mean crates becoming much more common or spawning in clusters, which is a larger change than the three icons and changes the pace of the road.

THE THREE ARE NOT SYMMETRIC. Repair matters only when damaged and NOS only when you would spend it, while TIME always matters - so at equal rarity the player learns to ignore two of the three. THE OWNER THEN FOUND THE HARDER HALF OF THIS AND IT IS WHAT THE UNIT ACTUALLY FIXED: "time isn't even always needed - if we're not in time mode, time is worthless." Time is not universally valuable at all. It is worth everything in one mode and nothing in another.

"NEVER SPAWNS" NEEDS A RULE FOR WHAT FILLS THE GAP. If NOS crates simply do not appear for a lorry then a lorry meets a third fewer pickups than a supercar, which quietly makes the production cars poorer as well as slower. The share would have to be redistributed rather than dropped.

AND A DEDICATED NOS CRATE REINSTATES RLG-107 IN A NARROWER FORM: a supercar with a full bottle drives over a NOS crate and gets nothing.

THE LEGIBILITY WAS THE REAL RISK, NOT THE CODE. A crate is a sprite drawn at 0.22 of the road's width and first seen at the draw edge, 35,000 units out. A wrench, a clock face and a bottle at that size are one grey smudge until you are nearly on it - and by then you have already committed to leaving the road for it. The suggestion made was to keep the white crate and colour the ICON, because colour survives the distance and the picture confirms it up close ([[RLG-044]] already rules colour a signal).

THE OWNER'S DECISION: ONE CRATE, EVERYTHING IT CAN GIVE. "It seems like the simplest solution is just to make the crate always just give you what you need. If timed mode is on, they will always give you 10 seconds. If you're damaged, they repair you up to 25%. If your NOS isn't full, you always get up to 25%."

EACH AWARD IS INDEPENDENT AND ALL OF THEM APPLY. That is the difference from RLG-107, which shipped a few hours earlier and paid seconds only as a FALLBACK when nothing else was worth anything. A damaged supercar on a timed run with a half-empty bottle now gets all three, and the label is built from the ones that were worth something.

AND THE FALLBACK WAS WRONG IN THE MODE IT MATTERED MOST, which is the owner's catch and the real defect here. `clockRuns()` is `mode === 'race' || timedRun`, so on a TEST DRIVE with TIMED off the clock does not count at all - and RLG-107 paid ten seconds into it anyway, as the reward for a car with nothing else to gain. A reward paid in a currency the mode does not use is exactly the invisible reward that ruling existed to remove. Seconds are paid only when the clock is running.

A CRATE WITH NOTHING TO GIVE IS NOW LEFT WHERE IT IS. Untimed, undamaged, and either no bottle or a full one: there is genuinely nothing to hand over. Consuming it silently would be the invisible reward again and would destroy something the player might want later, so it is not taken and the box stays on the shoulder. That case is small and it is the one part of this the owner has not ruled on.

PROVED by `tools/crate-test.py`, which gained the case the rule is about: with the clock turned off through `API.setTimed`, a damaged ROADSTER reads `REPAIRED -25%  NOS +25%` and the clock does not move; with it on, a damaged LORRY reads `REPAIRED -25%  +10 SEC`. Paying seconds regardless of the mode fails both untimed checks with the clock moving 10.00 in a mode that does not count it.

A CORRECTION, RECORDED BECAUSE IT WAS SAID TO THE OWNER. The reply that put those four points also claimed the proposal had already been tracked as a fragment. It had not; no fragment was written and the number was invented. This is that fragment.

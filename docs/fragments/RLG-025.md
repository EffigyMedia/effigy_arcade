### RLG-025 — Interstate: traffic can pile up and block the road completely
- **RULED 2026-08-28T13:51:40-04:00** — - **Owner report:** there must never be a pileup of traffic that completely blocks the road.

- **What exists already.** `spawnWave` has a `lanes.size < LANES` guard and a `gapX` concept, so
  waves are meant to leave a way through. The failure is presumably not at spawn but AFTER it:
  independent cars braking and drifting can close a gap that was open when it was created.

- **The fix has to be continuous, not just at spawn.** A guarantee checked once is not a guarantee.
- **RULED 2026-08-28T15:17:19-04:00** — - **The guarantee already existed and already ran.** `keepLaneOpen` is called every frame. It still
  let walls form, because it was asking the wrong question in the wrong places.

- **Counting lane indices is not measuring the road.** Cars drift inside a lane, and a shunt moves one
  bodily sideways while its `lane` field still says where it was assigned - so four cars can report
  four different lanes and leave no opening a car could fit through. What blocks a road is OCCUPIED
  WIDTH, so the corridor between occupied intervals is what is measured now.

- **A fixed bucket splits the wall it is looking for.** Cars at 1,499 and 1,501 landed in different
  `Math.round(z / 1500)` buckets, each of which then looked passable on its own, and the wall between
  them was never examined by either. The sweep is a sliding window stepped at half its width, so
  every stretch is seen whole by at least one window.

- **THE FIRST VERSION OF THE TEST WAS VACUOUS AND SAID SO.** With the correction switched off it
  still reported 0/180 blocked - it could not tell a working guarantee from a road that never crowds.
  A boolean was the wrong instrument. `tightestAhead` reports the NARROWEST corridor actually seen,
  and that discriminates: **0.306 with the fixer off, under the 0.34 limit.**

- **Which then showed the real fix was still wrong.** Reacting only once the corridor is already under
  a car width is too late - opening a gap takes a second or two of a car easing and moving over, and
  the player covers a lot of road in that time. The response now starts at a warning line of 0.62 and
  scales with how close the corridor is to the limit: a nudge at the top of the band, a car moving
  with intent at the bottom. **The road never arrives at the limit, which is the only way an absolute
  guarantee holds.**

- **One bug caught in review rather than by a test.** The moved car had its lane set to `-1` to stop
  the drift logic hauling it back into the wall. But `laneFree` matches on the lane index when
  choosing where to spawn, and a car carrying `-1` matches nothing - so a new car could have dropped
  straight on top of the one that had just moved over. It is reassigned to whichever lane it is now
  nearest instead.

- **Measured, with the fix:** narrowest corridor **0.452** lane units against a 0.34 requirement, 0 of
  180 samples blocked, over a 45-second run at speed. `drive-test` 21/21.

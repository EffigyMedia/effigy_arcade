### RLG-014 — The lamp is bolted over the hatch, and no machine has a controls screen
- **RULED 2026-08-28T12:57:03-04:00** — - **The emergency light stopped swinging and got bolted over the hatch.** It used to slide across the
  frame on a sine, which is a horror-film image and the wrong one for this room: **nothing else here
  moves.** There is no draught and no ship's roll to move it, so a light drifting back and forth read
  as a rectangle floating rather than as a lamp on a chain. The owner called it exactly that.

  Over the doorway it says what it is - an emergency light sits at the hatch it warns about - and it
  is drawn as a fitting rather than a bare rectangle: a housing with a lens, a wire guard across it,
  a bracket at each end, and a throw cast on the plating just under it. **The bracket is what makes
  it read as bolted TO something** instead of a shape sitting on top of the picture, and it is four
  pixels of work.

  It pulses on two sines of different periods with a floor under it. Those fittings are not strobes;
  they breathe, and the floor keeps the room from ever going fully dark.

- **THE CONTROLS SCREENS ARE GONE, ALL FOUR OF THEM.** This follows from [[RLG-002]] and the owner
  said so directly: these are touch games through and through. **A controls page exists to tell a
  player which keys do what. A thumb does not need to be told where it is**, and the pages listed
  gestures the interface already shows one tap away - a stick where you put your thumb, a throttle
  that stays where you leave it.

- **This reversed work from the same session, and that is worth recording rather than hiding.**
  CONTROLS had just been added to the three titles that lacked it, to match Hardpoint. The owner's
  ruling arrived while that was being verified. The right response was to delete all four screens,
  not to keep the ones that already existed - **a rule that applies to new work applies to old work,
  or it is not a rule.**

- **The check was inverted rather than deleted.** `tools/menu-test.py` asserted CONTROLS was present
  on every title; it asserts the absence now, and still walks OPTIONS in and back out, which is the
  path that could have broken when the controls screen was lifted out from between them.

- **What the screens carried besides key bindings was checked before deleting them.** Hardpoint's
  held the rule of the game - engines disable, everything else destroys, a wreck has no cargo - and
  that rule is already on its title in three words: HUNT, CRIPPLE, BOARD. Nothing was lost.

- **The rule is in the brief**: no machine has a controls screen and none should get one, and if a
  gesture ever needs explaining it is taught in play the first time it matters.

- **Proof.** `pack.sh` all gates, 41 files; `smoke-test` 6/6; `drive-test` 21/21; `menu-test` 4/4
  with the inverted assertion. The lamp was rendered and looked at.

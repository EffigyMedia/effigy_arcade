- **CLOSED 2026-09-01T15:42:01-04:00** - The bore is solid and the darkness ramps. Its opacity WAS the crossing - the tunnel was laid over the ordinary world with the place's own darkness as its alpha, so halfway through a transition the whole thing was at half opacity and the farmland showed through it. TWO THINGS WERE FUSED: how dark it is inside is a property of the place and should ramp; whether the walls exist is not a matter of degree. The crossing decides the EXTENT now - the tube is built from the tunnel's own mouth, which the event record already held to the world unit, and stops at the far portal. Measured across an approach: the mouth stands 27,564 units ahead, closes to 9,741, then reaches the camera, while the darkness ramps 0 to 1 over the same stretch - two numbers moving independently, which is the fix stated as a measurement. THE DARKNESS KEPT ITS REAL JOB and the check asserts BOTH halves, because a build that made the walls solid by freezing the ramp would have passed a check that only looked at the walls. TWO THINGS FOUND ON THE WAY: the debug biome setter placed a tunnel without arming its event, so the harness measured a bore beginning at the camera - a state the real game never produces - and the first version of the check sampled for two seconds when the car needs 37,000 units to reach the mouth, which the ramp check further up the same harness already carries a note about.

### UNT-142 - A fleet on the water: four kinds of boat, and they move

- Routing class: `feature` -> **claude-sonnet-5**, effort **medium**. Bias 1. Implementation to a spec that already answers the design questions. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened for: [[RLG-112]]
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-09-01T15:03:22-04:00** - The coastal completion. A fleet rather than one boat - sailboats, speedboats, fishing boats, liners and tankers - on both waters, and on the coast they are SCENERY on the seaward side at the owner's direction. The first attempt placed them by distance the way the bridge does and the owner read them as skyline detail, correctly: a coast's sea is at the ground's own level so it converges on the horizon, and every boat came out in a three-pixel band beside the headlands. THE REAL FIX IS THAT THE FLEET IS ONE TABLE. The bridge and the coast had two independent size tables, so correcting the coast's tanker to 330 metres left the bridge's at 117 - the same shape this project has been caught by three times. Every vessel is stated once in metres and knots and both waters convert through the car's own length. The speeds are the real knots and they give the owner's stated ordering for free, because that ordering is about the angular rate rather than the speed: tanker 0.88 pixels a second, sailboat 3.89, speedboat 35. The islands are clipped to the water side. The seaward check was rewritten rather than deleted, because the invariant it protected was never that the sea is empty but that no palm stands in it. NOT CLEAN: with the island clip in, coast-test failed about three runs in thirteen against one in twelve without it - three different checks, one of them the shoreline-join flake the audit already records, and the mechanism argues against the clip because it paints only above the horizon while the failing check samples below. Not isolated, and recorded rather than dismissed.

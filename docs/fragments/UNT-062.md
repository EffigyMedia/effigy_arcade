### UNT-062 - The packer regenerates, then verifies

- Routing class: `fix` -> **claude-sonnet-5**, effort **medium**. Bias 1. A known defect with a known cause. ([[RLG-092]])
- Opened by `unit.py`. **The identity exists before the first edit** ([[RLG-097]]), so the snapshot has somewhere to go.
- **CLOSED 2026-08-30T11:05:08-04:00** - The packer regenerates before it verifies. assets.js is generated and takes its version from sw.js, so in a build they agree by construction the moment the generator has run - but the comparison ran before it, so bumping sw.js failed the build that would have fixed it, and assets.js was seeded by hand once to get past it, which is the check introducing the drift it exists to catch. The check is not weakened: --check has nothing to regenerate and asks before, a build asks after, and there is now one definition of what agreement means. Proven by reproducing the fragment's own scenario: sw.js at v27 against assets.js at v26 makes --check refuse with the right message, and the build then succeeds and verifies at v27. The version was put back afterwards, because nothing shipping changed and a bump throws away every device's cache.

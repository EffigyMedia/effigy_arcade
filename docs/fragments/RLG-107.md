### RLG-107 — A car with no bottle is told it got no NOS
- **RULED 2026-08-31T10:50:58-04:00** — Owner, 2026-08-31: "For cars with no NOS, picking up the pickup says '+0 NOS', but that should be
omitted if they don't have NOS. Instead, it should just say '+N health' or however health is
expressed."

WHERE IT IS. The repair crate, in the pickup loop. It heals 25 and tops the bottle up by 25, then
writes one of two strings: `'REPAIRED \u2212' + healed + '%  NOS +' + gained` when there was damage,
or `'NOS +' + gained + '%'` when there was not. Neither branch asks whether the car HAS a bottle.
`hasNos()` already exists and is already consulted by the nitro button and by the gauges, so the
question is answerable one line away.

AND THE AWARD IS WRONG BEFORE THE TEXT IS. `nos = Math.min(100, nos + 25)` runs for every car,
including the ones with no bottle - so the fix is not only to hide a label. A car that cannot use
nitrous should not be given nitrous, and then the label follows from what actually happened rather
than being suppressed after the fact. Suppressing the text alone would leave a car carrying a charge
it can never spend.

THERE IS A THIRD CASE HIDING IN THIS, AND IT IS THE INTERESTING ONE. A car with no bottle, at full
health, driving over a crate now gets NOTHING - and the code comment beside the NOS award says
exactly why that matters: "On a clean run there is no damage to repair, so driving over one did
literally nothing - a reward that is invisible most of the time is not a reward." The bottle top-up
was added to solve that, and it does not solve it for the cars this report is about. So the ruling
needs an answer for what a no-bottle car at full health gets, and "nothing" is a real answer only if
somebody chooses it.

THE WORDING IS THE OWNER'S TO SETTLE, AND THEY SAID SO. Health is currently expressed as
`REPAIRED \u2212N%` - damage removed, shown as a negative because `dmg` counts up. The owner wrote
"'+N health' or however health is expressed I forget", which reads as a preference for the plain form
rather than an instruction to change it. Ask before renaming: `REPAIRED` also appears in the damage
HUD's own vocabulary, and two words for one quantity is the thing this project keeps having to undo.

CHECK THE OTHER TWO PICKUP PATHS WITH IT. The comment above the award says "the two other pickup paths
in this file both top the bottle up as well". If they do it unconditionally too, they have the same
defect and it should be fixed once rather than three times.
- **RULED 2026-08-31T17:36:20-04:00** — BUILT in v0.10.33, and the owner settled both of the questions this ruling left open.

FOUR PLACES GAVE NITROUS, NOT THREE. This ruling named the crate and said to check the other two pickup paths with it. There were four, and the fourth is the deepest: the repair crate, threading a roadblock gap, putting a cruiser out, and THE TRICKLE that refills the bottle over a run - `if(!nosOn && nos < 100) nos += dt * 1.1`, running for every car in the fleet whether or not it has a bottle. So a LORRY's charge climbed on its own for the whole run, and no pickup would ever have shown that.

All four go through `awardNos(n)` now, which returns what was ACTUALLY given so the caller can say so rather than guess. The award is fixed before the text is, which is what this ruling insisted on: suppressing the label alone would have left the car holding a charge it can never spend.

AND A CAR WITH NO BOTTLE NO LONGER STARTS A RUN HOLDING ONE. `reset` set `nos = 40` for everybody. It is `hasNos() ? 40 : 0` now, so a production car starts with nothing in a bottle it has not got rather than with a phantom charge that is merely invisible.

THE THIRD CASE IS ANSWERED: SECONDS. Owner's decision, 2026-08-31. A car with no bottle at full health driving over a crate got nothing, which is the exact problem the bottle top-up was added to solve - the code's own note says "a reward that is invisible most of the time is not a reward" - left unsolved for the half of the fleet the report is about. It is paid `CRATE_SECS` on the clock now, which is the currency Interstate is played in, the one a checkpoint already pays out, and needs no new system. THE RULE IS "NOTHING TO GAIN", NOT "NO BOTTLE": a supercar at full health with a full bottle is paid the same way, so a crate is never a thing you drive over for nothing.

THE WORDING STAYS AS IT WAS. Owner's decision: keep `REPAIRED −25%`. Worth recording that this ruling's caution about it no longer applies - it warned that `REPAIRED` also appears in the damage HUD's vocabulary and that two words for one quantity is what this project keeps having to undo. That HUD element is gone: damage is a visual effect now, `#dmgFill` is styled in the stylesheet and matches nothing, and this flash is the only place health is named anywhere in the game. There was nothing left to clash with, and the owner chose the existing wording on its own merits.

THE FLASH IS BUILT FROM WHAT HAPPENED. Each award is taken in turn and only the ones worth something appear, so the four cases read: `NOS +25%` for a supercar on a clean run, `REPAIRED −25%` for a damaged production car, `REPAIRED −25%  NOS +25%` for a damaged supercar, and `+10 SEC` for anything with nothing else to gain. The seconds use `timeAward`, which is the same function the checkpoint flash uses, so the two ways the clock goes up are worded identically by construction ([[RLG-106]]).

PROVED BY `tools/crate-test.py`, which is new. IT PARKS ITS OWN CRATE: crates spawn out of sight and sit on the SHOULDER at 0.86 to 1.02 of the road, so reaching one by driving would measure the spawner and the verge rather than the pickup. `API.parkCrate` puts one on the REAL crates array with the fields the spawner gives it, in front of the car, so the real pickup test runs on it - the same reason `parkTraffic` exists and says so.

IT CHECKS THE CAR AND NOT ONLY THE LABEL, which is the half that hiding the text would have passed. Measured: a ROADSTER gains 25 and is told `NOS +25%`; a LORRY and a VAN gain nothing, are told nothing about a bottle, and are paid 10 seconds; a damaged LORRY reads `REPAIRED −25%` and is NOT paid seconds as well, because the crate was already worth something; the bottle trickles on a ROADSTER over 2.5 seconds and does not move on a LORRY; and a run STARTED in a LORRY through the same restart a player gets begins holding 0 against the ROADSTER's 40.

Removing the gate by hand fails four checks with the exact reported text, `NOS +25%` on a LORRY.
- **RULED 2026-08-31T18:16:19-04:00** — PARTLY SUPERSEDED THE SAME DAY BY [[RLG-125]], and the part that went is the one this ruling was proudest of. The seconds paid to a car with nothing else to gain were a FALLBACK - only when there was no damage to repair and no bottle to fill - and the owner found the hole in it within hours: "time isn't even always needed - if we're not in time mode, time is worthless." `clockRuns()` is `mode === 'race' || timedRun`, so on a TEST DRIVE with TIMED off the clock does not count at all and those ten seconds bought nothing. A reward paid in a currency the mode does not use is precisely the invisible reward this ruling existed to remove, so the fix reproduced the fault it was fixing, one mode over. WHAT STANDS UNCHANGED: everything about the bottle. `awardNos` still gates all four paths, a car with no bottle is still given nothing and told nothing, and it still starts a run holding nothing. That half was the owner's report and it was right. WHAT CHANGED: the seconds are no longer a fallback and are paid whenever the clock is running, so every award is independent - which is simpler than what this ruling built as well as more correct.

### RLG-036 — Motorsport: slowing down on the circuit conjured civilian traffic
- **RULED 2026-08-28T15:32:11-04:00** — - **Owner report, from play:** stopping or going very slowly in Motorsport makes traffic start
  spawning behind you.

- **Confirmed, and it contradicted an answer I had just given.** In the same exchange I had listed
  the traffic work as Interstate-only on the grounds that `CFG.circuitOnly` gates civilian traffic.
  It gates four of the five spawners. It did not gate this one.

- **Why this one was missed, and it is a shape worth noticing.** Waves, police, roadblocks and crates
  are declared together further down `stepWorld`, all behind `roadFurniture`, where the gate is
  obvious to anyone editing near it. The fifth spawner - the one that drops a car in BEHIND you once
  you have been slower than the flow for two seconds - lives up with the SPEED logic, because that is
  where `slowFor` is accumulated. **A gate that is obvious in one place is invisible a hundred lines
  away**, and grouping by mechanism put this spawner among code that has nothing to do with the
  others.

- **`CFG.circuitOnly` is read directly rather than through `roadFurniture`.** That constant is
  declared further down the same function, so naming it at this point is a temporal dead zone and
  throws every frame.

- **NO EXISTING HARNESS COULD HAVE CAUGHT THIS.** `drive-test` holds the throttle down for thirty
  seconds; this only happens when you stop. That is the gap the owner's play found and the gates did
  not, and it is now closed: `tools/circuit-test.py` sits both games at a standstill for twelve
  seconds and counts. **The circuit must be empty and the highway must not be** - traffic arriving
  behind a stopped player is what makes stopping feel exposed on a motorway.

- **Measured:** Motorsport 0 civilian cars after twelve seconds stopped, Interstate 26.

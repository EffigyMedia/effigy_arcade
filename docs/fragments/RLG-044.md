### RLG-044 — Colour says what a car IS: racers saturated, player iridescent, traffic muted
- **RULED 2026-08-28T17:33:46-04:00** — **Owner, 2026-08-28:** "Only racers get the nice saturated colors, and in addition the player only
has the iridescent colors that are rewarded on the sports tournament. All traffic cars get more
muted color palettes. Sports cars that are part of traffic get these muted colors so they do not
read as racers."

**Colour is not decoration here. It is the readout that tells the player what a thing on the road
IS**, at a distance, in a fraction of a second, before any other cue arrives. Three classes:

| class | palette |
|---|---|
| racers | saturated |
| the player | iridescent, and those are a TOURNAMENT REWARD rather than a starting choice |
| all traffic | muted |

**The load-bearing case is the last sentence.** A tuner or a muscle car in traffic wears the muted
palette, because the fault being fixed is that a sports car in traffic reads as a rival. The engine
already has a note about this from the other direction - road.js:4055 records that after the
acceleration retune "a striped muscle car going past at 94 looks exactly like a rival" - so this is
the same confusion, and colour is the fix the owner has chosen for it.

**Consequence to settle before building:** the player's palette becomes a reward, so a new save has
no iridescent paint. What the player starts with is therefore an open question - saturated like a
racer, which they are, or something else. **Ask the owner.**

Touches `PAINT_KEYS`, `paintChoices()`, the garage, the tournament reward table, and the traffic
painters. It is a rule about which palette a painter draws from, not a change to any painter.
